## Source errors and inconsistencies (corrected value carried in the PDFs)
1. **Immunotherapy key points** — says sipuleucel-T is for mCRPC "with visceral metastases or cancer-related pain requiring opioids". The body text says the reverse. Correct: **without** them.
2. **Table number** — the table is printed as "TABLE 162.1"; the text cites Table 166.1. Correct: **Table 166.1**.
3. **"Dihydroxy testosterone"** — correct: dihydrotestosterone.
4. **COU-AA-302** — says the co-primary end points were "PFS and risk of radiographic progression". Correct: **rPFS and OS**.
5. **TAX 327** — says "95% CI 6–48%, P = 0.0005". Correct: HR 0.76, 95% CI 0.62–0.94 (a 6–38% reduction), **P = 0.009**.
6. **Enzalutamide interactions** — says strong or moderate CYP3A4/CYP2C8 inducers "increase the AUC". Correct: inducers **decrease** exposure.
7. **Bone remodelling** — says "absorption caused by osteoblasts". Correct: **osteoclasts**.
8. **VISION** — "mean OS" should read **median** OS. The Fig. 166.9 citation "N Engl J Med 285" should read **385**.
9. **TheraP** — "screened … to ensure PSA expression". Correct: **PSMA** expression.
10. **PSMA** — "carboxypeptidate" should read carboxypeptidase.
11. **GALAHAD** — describes niraparib improving rPFS "compared to placebo". GALAHAD was a **single-arm** phase II, so there was no placebo arm. The notes carry only the BRCA-cohort rPFS of 8.2 months.
12. **MAGNITUDE** — lists survival, time to chemotherapy and delayed progression as the primary end points. Correct: the primary end point was **rPFS**. "No benefit of niraparib in addition to ADT" should read "in addition to **abiraterone**".
13. **TRITON3** — says it "included 855 patients", but 270 + 135 = **405 randomised**.
14. **TRITON2** — says ORR was 43.5% "in those with disease" and 50.8% "in those without the disease". Both figures are for measurable disease: **43.5% by independent review** and **50.8% by investigator**.
15. **"TRITON I"** — this describes the post-ARPI, post-taxane BRCA population and cites the same paper as TRITON2 (Abida 2020). It is most likely a duplicate of TRITON2 under the wrong name, so it is summarised once, as TRITON2.
16. **TOPARP-A** — the olaparib study described first (33%/88% response, Mateo 2015) and "TOPARP-A" are the same trial, and the breakthrough designation is described twice. Summarised once.
17. **ALSYMPCA** — the text gives 809 patients; Fig. 166.8 gives 921 (614:307). The 809 is the interim analysis and the 921 is the full randomised population. Both are tabulated in the notes.
18. **TALAPRO-2** — for the HRR-deficient group, the text gives "not reached vs 13.8 mo, HR 0.45" and Fig. 166.10B gives "27.9 vs 16.4 mo, HR 0.46". The text is the dedicated HRR-deficient cohort at a later data cut; the figure is the subgroup within the all-comers cohort. Both are tabulated.
19. **PROpel approval** — dated "June 2023" in the source. The FDA approval of olaparib + abiraterone was 31 May 2023. The notes say "2023".
20. **ARV-766** — "AR L702" is missing a letter. Correct: **L702H**.
21. **AFFIRM** — "the FDA approved enzalutamide in for …" is missing the year.
22. **PTEN** — expanded as "protein tyrosine phosphatase (PTEN)". Correct: phosphatase and tensin homologue.
23. **Radium-223 + abiraterone** — "black label warning" is ambiguous. The notes say "label warning against the combination".
24. **Bone-targeted section** — refers to neuroendocrine prostate cancer "(see later)", but it is covered earlier.
25. **Denosumab trial** — 1904 enrolled vs 951 + 950 = 1901 in the arms. Minor; left as is.
26. **Ipilimumab** — "radiologic tumor responses … in about 5% of patients ( )": an empty citation (text dropped). The claim is kept.
27. Typos with no effect on content: "zolendronic", "mCPRC", "metastatic CPRC", "Effective anticancer agents such of".

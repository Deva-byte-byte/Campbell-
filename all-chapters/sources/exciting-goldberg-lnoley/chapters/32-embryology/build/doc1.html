Chapter 32 — Embryology of the Human Genitourinary Tract — Numbers, Gold Standards and TrapsSource pp. 584–607 only (partial coverage): the rest of bladder/ureter, kidney and closing sections were not supplied

If you read nothing else

  • SRY (sex-determining region Y), short arm of Y → Sertoli cells → testis; no SRY → ovary by default
  • Sertoli → Müllerian-inhibiting substance (MIS) (Müllerian regression, weeks 8–10); Leydig → testosterone from 8 weeks
  • Type 2 5α-reductase (testosterone → dihydrotestosterone, DHT): needed for penis + prostate; deficiency → severe hypospadias, rudimentary prostate
  • Hypospadias ≈ 1 in 250 boys; commonest meatus at shaft–glans junction; developmental arrest
  • Only fold fusion is androgen-dependent; plate canalisation is androgen-independent
  • Plication at 12 o'clock: nerve-free zone + thickest tunica (dorsal nerves 11 and 1 o'clock)
  • Cryptorchidism ≈ 3% newborn males; hydrocele / hernia more common
  • Streak gonad 46,XY → remove (gonadoblastoma); all streaks → hormone replacement
  • Entire vaginal epithelium + hymen = urogenital sinus epithelium (UGE) (Bulmer); imperforate hymen = commonest vaginal obstruction
  • Weigert–Meyer: upper-pole orifice caudal + medial → ectopia; lower-pole lateral → reflux

External genitalia

  • Indifferent stage 8–9 weeks; development mostly complete by end of 2nd trimester
  • First sign of masculinisation: ↑ anogenital distance → tubercle elongation → urethral plate
  • Germ layers: ectoderm = skin / prepuce; mesoderm = corpora; endoderm = urethral plate
  • Whole urethral epithelium = endoderm (urogenital sinus, UGS); meatus = ectoderm–endoderm junction
  • Double zipper: canalisation (proliferation, no apoptosis) → fold fusion (median raphe)
  • Timetable: coronal sulcus 11–12 wk; folds fused 16 wk; penis complete 18 wk; fetal chordee gone 20 wk
  • Shaft urethra = fusion; glanular urethra = direct canalisation
  • Glenister = outdated ectodermal-intrusion theory of glanular urethra
  • Hypospadias = 4 components: ectopic meatus, dorsal hooded foreskin, ventral skin deficiency, ± curvature
  • Hypospadias aetiology: mostly unknown; genetic susceptibility + endocrine disruptors
  • Hypospadiac penis normal except urethra, spongiosum, ventral skin, ventral prepuce
  • Hooded foreskin: prepuce absent ventrally, excess dorsally
  • Leydig cells + testosterone: 8 weeks
  • Nerves: somatic S2–S4 pudendal via Alcock's canal; sympathetic L1–L2; parasympathetic S2–S4
  • Neural crest → peripheral / autonomic nerves at week 5
  • Cavernous nerves exit between transverse perineal muscles and membranous urethra
  • At 16 weeks: penis ≈ 90° to body; clitoris recessed to body wall
  • Duplicated urethra: plate splits (complete from bladder; Y along shaft); Sonic hedgehog
  • Clitoris = penis without urethra; vestibular folds never fuse; 12 o'clock nerve-free zone too
  • Congenital adrenal hyperplasia (CAH): autosomal recessive, 46,XX virilised; urethra may be male-identical
  • CAH clues: impalpable gonads + hyperpigmentation; mimics hypospadias + bilateral impalpable testes

Prostate and seminal vesicle

  • Prostatic buds 10 weeks (9.5–10) from UGE below bladder; branching from ~11 weeks
  • Buds mainly lateral to verumontanum; budding stochastic — fetal ventral buds lost
  • Six stages: prebud → solid buds → branching → canalisation → luminal / basal → secretion
  • FOXA1 = endodermal (UGE) marker
  • Verumontanum = Wolffian ducts + utricle (mesoderm) meet UGE (endoderm); uniquely human
  • Canalisation proximal → distal; smooth muscle ventral first, then lateral / dorsal
  • DHT ≈ 10× androgen receptor (AR) affinity of testosterone; 5αR2 mainly in mesenchyme
  • Non-functional AR → no prostate; female UGS + androgen → prostate
  • 5αR1 normal in 5αR2 deficiency — does not compensate
  • Jost and Price: animal studies establishing androgen dependence
  • Ductal length to first branch ≈ 250 µm (14 wk) → 1000 µm (17 wk)
  • Urogenital sinus mesenchyme (UGM) instructs epithelium — induces prostate even in bladder epithelium
  • Table 32.1: basal = TP63, KRT5/14, KRT19, GSTpi; luminal = KRT8/18
  • Oestrogen (DES) → squamous metaplasia via ESR1 (oestrogen receptor α), needed in stroma and epithelium
  • Seminal vesicle = Wolffian diverticulum 10–12 wk; side ducts months 4–5; innervated 13 wk; main anomaly = cyst

Gonads and gonadal descent

  • Primordial germ cells (PGCs): yolk sac week 3 → hindgut dorsal mesentery → ridge; germinal ridge 4 wk
  • Primary sex cords week 6; testicular cords ~6 wk; ovarian cortex / medulla ~8 wk
  • Gonads look identical 7–8 wk; then testis spherical, ovary oblong
  • Ovary = default: no SRY → no Sertoli cells, MIS, Leydig cells or androgen
  • Seminiferous cords appear to come from mesonephric tubules, not primary sex cords
  • Efferent ductules = 5–12 mesonephric tubules; full tubule–epididymis continuity by 3rd month
  • Testosterone control: placental chorionic gonadotrophin early → fetal pituitary later
  • Wolffian differentiation weeks 8–12 → epididymis, vas, seminal vesicle
  • Remnants: appendix testis + utricle = Müllerian; appendix epididymis + paradidymis = Wolffian / mesonephric
  • Germ cell nests 10–11 wk (2–4 cells); primary follicle ~20 wk; secondary at term; antral + theca postnatal
  • Oocytes arrest in meiosis I from fetal life to puberty
  • Homologues: Cowper ↔ Bartholin; Littre ↔ Skene; spongiosum ↔ vestibular bulb; Gartner duct = female Wolffian remnant
  • Transabdominal phase weeks 10–15: cranial suspensory ligament (CSL) regresses (androgen); gubernacular swelling (androgen-independent; INSL3)
  • Inguinoscrotal phase from 7th month: androgen via genitofemoral nerve CGRP (calcitonin gene-related peptide); migration 3–5 cm
  • INSL3 (insulin-like peptide 3, 1993): null mice = isolated intra-abdominal cryptorchidism
  • Operate early: early orchidopexy restores fertility potential (Insl3-null mice)
  • Gubernacular cord transection → contralateral canal or aberrant intra-abdominal testis
  • Both gonads in pelvis by month 3; testis in scrotum ~month 7; ovary on broad ligament month 3
  • Female gubernaculum → ovarian ligament + round ligament; patent processus → indirect hernia
  • Anomaly map: CSL retained → intra-abdominal; failed migration → congenital cryptorchidism; failed closure → hydrocele / hernia; failed involution → ascending testis
  • Streak gonad: 45,X (Turner) / 46,XX / 46,XY; Müllerian retained, Wolffian regressed
  • Ovotesticular: ducts follow ipsilateral gonad; female fertility possible, no spermatogenesis reported
  • Persistent Müllerian duct syndrome (PMDS): 46,XY + uterus / tubes ("hernia uteri inguinale"); usually MIS receptor defect; found at exploration for bilateral abdominal testes
  • Müllerian sensitivity window to MIS 9–13 weeks

Müllerian structures

  • Müllerian invagination start week 5; Wolffian duct = essential guidewire
  • Ducts between Wolffian ducts weeks 7–8; fuse week 8 (uterovaginal canal); septum gone week 9
  • Indifferent stage ≈ 54 days (Carnegie stage 22)
  • Uterine tube = cranial unfused Müllerian ducts; fimbriae from ostia
  • Endocervix simple columnar; exocervix stratified squamous
  • Markers: PAX2 = Müllerian; FOXA1 = UGE; switch abruptly at vaginal–cervical junction
  • Vaginal canalisation ~15 wk → mostly complete 18–19 wk; glycogenated by 21 wk
  • Koff = mixed, Müllerian-predominant; Bulmer = all UGE → supported by immunohistochemistry
  • Mouse is opposite: Müllerian epithelium replaces sinus vagina
  • Transverse vaginal septum ≈ 1 in 80,000; middle / upper third junction (failed canalisation)
  • Didelphys = failed fusion; unicornuate = one duct absent; septum persists → obstruction / duplication
  • Obstructed hemi-vagina and ipsilateral renal anomaly (OHVIRA): post-menarche cyclic pain + discharge; may present as acute abdomen / tubo-ovarian abscess
  • OHVIRA kidney usually dysplastic / atrophic ± ectopic ureter; true agenesis = absent hemi-trigone

Bladder: ureteral development

  • Urorectal septum divides cloaca weeks 5–6 → dorsal anorectal canal + ventral UGS (Rathke, Tourneux)
  • Allantois → urachal remnant
  • Anorectal malformations from cloacal membrane, not urorectal septum
  • Ureterovesical junction inserts into trigone identically in both sexes
  • Wolffian entry to cloaca = landmark: vesicourethral canal (bladder, pelvic urethra) vs caudal UGS (phallic urethra; female urethra + vestibule)
  • Common excretory ducts join UGS weeks 5–6; apoptosis separates ureter from mesonephric duct
  • Trigone mostly bladder smooth muscle; orifices migrate cranially + laterally
  • Bud too low → lateral orifice → reflux; too high → caudal / medial → ectopic
  • Ectopic sites: male — bladder neck, verumontanum, vas; female — Gartner duct cyst, vestibule
  • Prostate below ureteric insertion → larger male prostatic urethra

Classic traps

  • Trap: glanular urethra ectodermal — Actually: whole urethra endodermal (UGS)
  • Trap: hypospadias = deformation — Actually: developmental arrest
  • Trap: MIS from Leydig cells — Actually: Sertoli; Leydig make testosterone
  • Trap: appendix testis Wolffian — Actually: Müllerian; appendix epididymis is Wolffian
  • Trap: gubernacular swelling needs androgen — Actually: androgen-independent; CSL regression is dependent
  • Trap: ascending testis congenital — Actually: acquired; processus fails to involute
  • Trap: PMDS = absent MIS — Actually: usually MIS receptor defect
  • Trap: OHVIRA = renal agenesis — Actually: renal anomaly, usually dysplasia; check for ectopic ureter before septum resection
  • Trap: adult upper vagina Müllerian — Actually: UGE replaces it up to cervix
  • Trap: fetal prostatic squamous metaplasia pathological — Actually: normal, maternal oestrogen

### MÜLLERIAN STRUCTURES (FEMALE INTERNAL GENITALIA)

- **Why males lack Müllerian structures:** MIS from the testis.
- **Why females keep them.** Females have **no testosterone**, so the **Wolffian ducts resorb**. The Müllerian ducts persist and form the **uterine body, cervix and fallopian (uterine) tubes**.
- **In whole mounts**, the **uterine body, cervix and vagina cannot be told apart**. The uterus enlarges with gestational age.

#### Development of the Human Female Reproductive Tract

**Müllerian duct formation — sequence**
1. **Start of week 5:** coelomic epithelium **invaginates on the lateral surface of the urogenital ridge**. These infoldings become the **tubal ostia**.
2. The paired Müllerian (paramesonephric) ducts **grow caudally using the Wolffian ducts as guidewires**. **The Wolffian duct is essential** for Müllerian duct migration to the UGS.
3. The relationship between the ducts changes along their length:
   - **cranially:** the ducts are separated by mesenchyme;
   - **further down:** their **basement membranes** are in contact;
   - **at the tip:** the **epithelia** are in direct contact.
   - This contact once suggested that cells cross between the two ducts, but **lineage tracing in mice does not support this**.
4. **Weeks 7–8:** the Müllerian ducts lie **between the two Wolffian ducts** as they approach the UGS.
   - The route has two gentle curves (horizontal and vertical parts).
   - Their point of contact is the **Müllerian tubercle**, a poorly defined term of limited value.
5. **Week 8:** the ducts **fuse in the midline → the uterovaginal canal**. A **midline epithelial septum** forms.
6. **Week 9:** the **septum disappears**.
7. **The female Wolffian ducts then degenerate.** Their remnants are:
   - **epoöphoron and paroöphoron** in the broad ligament;
   - **Gartner's duct** in the vaginal wall.

**Uterine tube.**
- **Origin:** the paired **cranial, unfused** parts of the Müllerian ducts.
- **Fimbriae** come from the irregular ostia.
- **At 8 weeks:** the tube is narrow, with a circular epithelial profile.
- **Changes in the following months:**
  - the **mucosa becomes highly folded**, most of all in the **infundibulum and ampulla**;
  - the mesenchyme divides into an **inner stroma** and an **outer smooth muscle layer**.

**Uterine corpus and cervix.**
- **Origin:** the **cranial uterovaginal canal**. The caudal canal takes part in vaginal development.
- **Septum anomalies.** A midline septum marks the zone of fusion and is normally eliminated. **If it fails to regress → malformations** (obstruction or duplication).
- **Corpus epithelium** stays **simple columnar** and forms the **uterine and cervical glands**.
- **Cervical epithelium:**
  - **endocervix:** simple columnar and glandular;
  - near the **external os**, the Müllerian epithelium becomes **stratified squamous**, and this also covers the **exocervix**.
- **Mesenchyme** forms the **endometrial stroma**, surrounded by the **myometrium**.

**Vagina — origin of the epithelium**

- **The debate:** proposed contributions have been from the UGS, Müllerian ducts or Wolffian ducts, alone or in combination.

| | Koff | Bulmer | Immunohistochemistry (this chapter) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Proposal | **Müllerian and UGE (sinovaginal bulbs)**, with **Müllerian predominance** | **UGE upgrowth forms the entire vaginal epithelial lining** | **Supports Bulmer** |
| Method | Haematoxylin and eosin (H&E) only | H&E only | **PAX2** (Müllerian marker) vs **FOXA1** (endodermal UGE marker) |

**Sequence of vaginal development (the PAX2/FOXA1 evidence)**
1. **Weeks 8–9:** the Müllerian ducts and uterovaginal canal are **PAX2-positive**.
2. **Caudal proliferation.** The caudal uterovaginal canal proliferates into the **solid vaginal plate**, which abuts the **FOXA1-positive sinovaginal bulbs** (UGS-derived).
3. **12 weeks:**
   - the vaginal plate is continuous with the uterine end;
   - most of the plate is **PAX2-positive**;
   - **FOXA1** is limited to the caudal plate, urethra and bladder.
4. **Weeks 14–21:** the plate lengthens, and **FOXA1-positive UGE progressively replaces PAX2-positive Müllerian epithelium**.
5. **Canalisation** of the vaginal plate starts at **about 15 weeks** and is **mostly complete by 18–19 weeks**.
   - The fornices are defined, and the **exocervix projects into the vault**.
   - At this point FOXA1 has reached **mid-vagina**, and PAX2 runs continuously from the tube to the upper vagina.
6. **21 weeks:**
   - the vaginal epithelium is **multilayered and glycogenated**, likely from **endogenous oestrogen**;
   - **FOXA1 runs from the introitus to the cervix**;
   - **PAX2 covers the tube, uterus and cervix**;
   - the two markers switch **abruptly at the vaginal–cervical junction**.

- **Conclusions:**
  - **the entire human vaginal epithelium is UGE-derived**;
  - the **hymen** is FOXA1-positive, so it is presumed to be **UGE-derived**.

**Human vs mouse vagina**

| | Human | Mouse |
|---|---|---|
| Final vaginal epithelium | **UGE (FOXA1)**, replacing Müllerian epithelium **cranially up to the cervix** | **Müllerian (PAX2)**, replacing the endodermal sinus vagina **postnatally** |
| Terminology | — | "Müllerian vagina" (cranial) and "sinus vagina" (caudal) |
| Direction of replacement | **Sinus replaces Müllerian** | **Müllerian replaces sinus** |

**Upper vagina — two statements in the chapter**

| Statement | Where | What it refers to |
|---|---|---|
| Müllerian ducts give rise to the "upper vagina" | Fig. 32.25 legend; streak gonad text | Classic teaching: the **Müllerian (uterovaginal canal) origin of the upper vaginal plate** |
| Entire vaginal epithelium is UGE-derived | Vagina text, Key Points, Table 32.3 | The **final epithelial lining**, after UGE replaces Müllerian epithelium |

- **Reconciling them:** the **upper vagina starts as Müllerian epithelium and is re-lined by UGE**. The chapter's own conclusion concerns the **epithelium**.

**Concept —** *The female tract is built by **Müllerian ducts that depend on the Wolffian duct to reach the UGS, then fuse, then lose their septum**. Each step has a failure mode, and the vagina is the exception whose lining is **ultimately replaced by the sinus**.*

### Clinical Correlation: Vaginal Agenesis and Imperforate Hymen

- **Transverse vaginal septum:**
  - caused by **failure of the vaginal canal to canalise** → atresia or obstruction;
  - incidence about **1 in 80,000 females**;
  - typically at the **junction of the middle and upper thirds**.
- **Imperforate hymen:**
  - caused by **failure of the inferior end to open**;
  - it is the **commonest cause of vaginal obstruction**.

### Clinical Correlation: Uterus Didelphys

- **Uterus didelphys:** **failure of fusion** of the caudal Müllerian ducts.
- **Unicornuate uterus:** **one Müllerian duct fails to develop**.

**Müllerian anomalies — failed step → anomaly**

| Failed step | Anomaly |
|---|---|
| One duct fails to develop | **Unicornuate uterus** |
| Ducts fail to fuse | **Uterus didelphys** |
| Septum fails to regress | **Obstruction or duplication** (septate malformations) |
| Vaginal plate fails to canalise | **Transverse vaginal septum** (middle/upper third junction) |
| Inferior end fails to open | **Imperforate hymen** (commonest obstruction) |
| Müllerian and Wolffian ducts both abnormal | **OHVIRA** |

### Clinical Correlation: Obstructed Hemi-Vagina and Unilateral Renal Anomalies

- **OHVIRA:** a rare combined **Müllerian and urinary tract anomaly**, due to abnormal development of **both the Müllerian and Wolffian ducts**.
- **Presentation:**
  - usually **after menarche**, with **cyclic abdominal pain and vaginal discharge**;
  - sometimes **acutely**, with an **acute abdomen or tubo-ovarian abscess**.
- **The renal anomaly is not always agenesis.** Cases have been reported with **ipsilateral dysplastic kidneys or ectopic ureters**.
- **Why the kidney is often not seen on imaging:** usually **dysplasia and atrophy** from abnormal (not absent) Wolffian duct formation, **not true agenesis**.

**True renal agenesis vs OHVIRA renal anomaly**

| | True renal agenesis | OHVIRA renal anomaly |
|---|---|---|
| Cause | **Absent** Wolffian duct, ureteric bud or metanephric blastema | **Abnormal** Wolffian duct with abnormal ipsilateral Müllerian duct |
| Kidney | Absent | **Dysplastic or atrophic nubbin**, may drain via an **ectopic ureter** |
| Associated finding | **Absent ipsilateral hemi-trigone** | Ectopic ureter may enter the obstructed hemi-vagina |

- **Why the distinction matters.** A dysplastic kidney with an ectopic ureter can cause **persistent vaginal leakage after resection of the vaginal septum**. So **do not assume agenesis**.
- **Terminology:** the "RA" in OHVIRA is now **"ipsilateral renal anomalies"**, not "renal agenesis".

### KEY POINTS: MÜLLERIAN DEVELOPMENT

- **Vaginal epithelium.** Immunohistochemistry supports **Bulmer**: the entire human vaginal epithelium is **UGE-derived**, with no Müllerian or Wolffian contribution.
- **The midline septum** marks the fusion zone and is normally eliminated. **Failure → uterine obstruction or duplication.**
- **Imperforate hymen** is the **commonest cause of vaginal obstruction**.

### BLADDER: URETERAL DEVELOPMENT

- **Shared wall structure of bladder and ureter:**
  - urothelium-lined lumen;
  - **lamina propria** of extracellular matrix with disorganised smooth muscle;
  - outer **thick smooth muscle bundles**;
  - serosa.
- **Sex differences are small.** The ureters and bladder develop alike and are of similar size in both sexes.
  - The exception is in males: **the prostate develops below the ureteric insertion**, so the **prostatic urethra is larger** than the female homologue.
- **The ureterovesical junction inserts into the trigone identically in both sexes.**

#### Formation of Urogenital Sinus

- **Division of the cloaca.** At **weeks 5–6**, the **urorectal septum** divides the **cloaca** into:
  - the **dorsal anorectal canal**;
  - the **ventral UGS**.
  - This is based on the classic observations of Rathke and Tourneux.
- **Anorectal malformations** are thought to arise from the **cloacal membrane, not the urorectal septum**.
- **The UGS and its diverticulum, the allantois,** form the bladder and urethra. The **allantois is the precursor of the urachal remnant**; early on, the bladder is continuous with it.
- **The Wolffian ducts join the cloaca** just before it divides. **Their entry point is the landmark** between:
  - the **cephalad vesicourethral canal** → **bladder and pelvic urethra**;
  - the **caudal UGS** → **phallic urethra in males**, and **urethra and vaginal vestibule in females**.

#### Formation of Trigone

- **Weeks 5–6:** the **common excretory ducts** (the mesonephric ducts distal to the ureteric bud origin) **dilate and join the UGS**.
- **Apoptosis** lets the **ureters disconnect from the mesonephric ducts and fuse with the bladder**.

**Origin of the trigone — classic vs current view**

| | Classic hypothesis | Current (mouse lineage tracing) |
|---|---|---|
| Fibromuscular wall of the trigone | **Extension of ureteral tissue** into the bladder | **Mostly bladder smooth muscle**; only a **minor ureteral contribution** |

- **Orifice migration.** The ureteral orifices are incorporated into the bladder and **migrate cranially and laterally** within the bladder base.

**Weigert–Meyer rule** *(inferred mainly from duplex kidneys)*
- **Rule:** the **upper-pole orifice rotates dorsally** relative to the lower-pole orifice and comes to lie **more caudal and medial**.

| | Lower-pole ureter | Upper-pole ureter |
|---|---|---|
| Bud origin when abnormal | **Too low** on the mesonephric duct | **Too high** on the mesonephric duct |
| Incorporation | **Premature**, with excess migration | Delayed or incomplete |
| Orifice position | **Lateral** (and cranial) | **Caudal and medial**, i.e. ectopic |
| Consequence | **Vesicoureteral reflux**: short intramural tunnel | **Ectopic insertion** |
| Ectopic sites, male | — | **Bladder neck, verumontanum**, or **Wolffian derivatives such as the vas deferens** |
| Ectopic sites, female | — | **Wolffian remnants (e.g. Gartner duct cyst)** or the **vaginal vestibule** |

- **Ectopic vas deferens.** It results from anomalous Wolffian development. The **vas can join the ureter** instead of the verumontanum, and both then drain into the prostatic urethra via a common… *(the text breaks here; pending)*

**Concept —** *Where the ureteric bud arises on the Wolffian duct fixes where its orifice ends up. **Too low → lateral orifice → reflux; too high → caudal and medial orifice → ectopia into Wolffian territory.***

## The numbers to lock in

**Key facts, figures and cut-offs**
- **Müllerian duct invagination:** start of week 5.
- **Müllerian ducts between the Wolffian ducts:** weeks 7–8.
- **Fusion into the uterovaginal canal:** week 8. **Septum gone:** week 9.
- **Indifferent stage:** about 54 days (Carnegie stage 22).
- **Vaginal canalisation:** starts about 15 weeks, mostly complete by 18–19 weeks. **Glycogenated epithelium:** 21 weeks.
- **Transverse vaginal septum:** about 1 in 80,000; at the junction of the middle and upper thirds.
- **Cloaca divided by the urorectal septum:** weeks 5–6. **Common excretory ducts join the UGS:** weeks 5–6.
- **Markers:** **PAX2** = Müllerian; **FOXA1** = endodermal (UGE).

**Commonest / key**
- **Imperforate hymen:** commonest cause of vaginal obstruction.
- **OHVIRA:** presents after menarche with cyclic pain and discharge; can present as an acute abdomen or tubo-ovarian abscess.
- **Vaginal epithelium:** entirely UGE; hymen UGE.

**Eponyms**
- **Koff (1933):** vaginal epithelium mixed, Müllerian-predominant.
- **Bulmer (1957):** vaginal epithelium entirely UGE — **supported by immunohistochemistry**.
- **Weigert–Meyer rule:** in duplex kidneys, the upper-pole orifice is caudal and medial (prone to ectopia) and the lower-pole orifice is lateral (prone to reflux).
- **Gartner duct / cyst:** Wolffian remnant; a site for ectopic upper-pole ureter insertion in females.
- **Rathke and Tourneux:** classic descriptions of the urorectal septum.
- **Müllerian tubercle:** where the Müllerian ducts meet the UGS; a term of limited value.

**When to operate / when not to assume**
- **OHVIRA: do not assume renal agenesis.** A dysplastic kidney with an ectopic ureter causes persistent vaginal leakage after resection of the septum.

**Classic traps**
- **Trap:** the upper vaginal epithelium is Müllerian in the adult. **Actually:** UGE replaces it up to the cervix; Müllerian origin is only transient.
- **Trap:** human and mouse vaginas form alike. **Actually:** they form in opposite ways — the human sinus replaces the Müllerian epithelium; in the mouse, Müllerian replaces sinus.
- **Trap:** the Müllerian ducts migrate independently. **Actually:** the Wolffian duct is a required guidewire.
- **Trap:** OHVIRA = renal agenesis. **Actually:** the "RA" now means renal anomalies; dysplasia or atrophy is usual.
- **Trap:** true agenesis and OHVIRA look the same on the trigone. **Actually:** true agenesis has an absent ipsilateral hemi-trigone.
- **Trap:** the trigone is ureteral tissue. **Actually:** it is mostly bladder smooth muscle.
- **Trap:** the abnormally placed lower-pole orifice lies caudally. **Actually:** it lies laterally and refluxes; the upper-pole orifice lies caudally and medially and is ectopic.
- **Trap:** anorectal malformations come from the urorectal septum. **Actually:** they come from the cloacal membrane.

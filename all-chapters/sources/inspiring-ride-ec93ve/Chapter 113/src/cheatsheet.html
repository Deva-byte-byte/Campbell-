Chapter 113 — Pharmacologic Management of Lower Urinary Tract Storage and Emptying Failure — Numbers, Gold Standards and Traps

If you read nothing else

  • Solifenacin 5 mg fails → add mirabegron 50 mg (SYNERGY, BESIDE)
  • BESIDE: combination > solifenacin 5 mg, not > solifenacin 10 mg
  • Mirabegron fails → add antimuscarinic (MILAI II, 52 weeks)
  • Mirabegron add-on tested only with solifenacin
  • Combination adds constipation; no heart rate, blood pressure (BP), QT signal
  • Vibegron + antimuscarinic: phase IIb only
  • Resistant nocturia → α-blocker + desmopressin
  • DIGNITY: onabotulinumtoxinA (onabotA) 200 U equal in anticholinergic users vs non-users
  • Systemic α-agonists for stress urinary incontinence (SUI): US Food and Drug Administration (FDA) ban, December 2003
  • Intraprostatic onabotA = saline in large randomised controlled trials (RCTs)

Combination Therapy

  • Rationale: different mechanisms → use when antimuscarinic gives intolerable adverse events (AEs) or insufficient response
  • SYNERGY (12 weeks), SYNERGY II (12 months): solifenacin 5 + mirabegron 50 > each monotherapy for incontinence (UI) and voids
  • BESIDE continence: 46.0% combination vs 37.9% solifenacin 5 vs 40.2% solifenacin 10
  • BESIDE dry mouth: highest with solifenacin 10 → combination = alternative to dose escalation
  • BESIDE age subanalysis: benefit at ≥65 and ≥75 years
  • MILAI: mirabegron 25 → 50 mg added after ≥4 weeks solifenacin 2.5/5 mg
  • MILAI II add-ons: solifenacin 5, propiverine 20, imidafenacin 0.2, tolterodine extended release (ER) 4 mg
  • MILAI II titration: dose doubled after 8 weeks, except tolterodine ER
  • Vibegron/tolterodine 100/4 mg → ↓micturitions, urgency vs monotherapies
  • Nocturnal polyuria (NP): ~30% of men ≥60 years with benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH)
  • α-blocker-resistant BPH + desmopressin → ≥50% fall in nocturnal voids in most
  • Desmopressin 0.1 mg oral, age ≥65: ↓nocturnal voids and volume; no placebo control
  • Tolterodine after 12 weeks vaginal estradiol → benefit; reverse order → none
  • Solifenacin ± vaginal oestrogen RCT: no difference
  • Vaginal estradiol ring ≈ oral oxybutynin
  • DIGNITY: neurogenic detrusor overactivity (NDO), spinal cord injury (SCI) or multiple sclerosis (MS), 200 U
  • EMBARK: idiopathic overactive bladder (OAB), 100 U, all anticholinergic-refractory → no user comparison

Drugs That Failed in Clinical Trials to Improve Storage or Voiding Function

  • Vitamin D3: ↓urgency incontinence (UUI) only; obesity blunts effect
  • Elocalcitol 75/150 µg (phase IIb): ↓incontinence; first-contraction volume unchanged
  • Calcium channel blockers (verapamil, nimodipine 30 mg): no storage benefit
  • Cardiovascular calcium channel blockers: no reported impaired bladder contractility
  • K+ channel openers: underdosed to avoid cardiovascular AEs; ZD0947 25 mg null
  • Flupirtine (KCNQ/Kv7): trial halted → hepatotoxicity
  • URO-902: BK channel α-subunit gene therapy; phase 1 only
  • PSD503 (phenylephrine 20% vaginal gel): ↓pad weight; poor acceptance
  • Phenylpropanolamine: non-selective → hypertension, haemorrhagic stroke
  • Clenbuterol 20 µg twice daily: tremor, tachycardia; unbalanced baselines
  • Botulinum toxin B: ≤3 months at highest dose; ≤10 weeks at 5,000 U

Drugs That Failed to Improve Voiding Dysfunction

  • Prostatic onabotA RCT entry: International Prostate Symptom Score (IPSS) >12, peak flow (Qmax) 5–15 mL/s; or IPSS >14, Qmax 4–15
  • Prostatic onabotA: 100–300 U, no dose response; phase II urosepsis
  • PRX302: prostate-specific antigen (PSA)-activated proaerolysin, 0.6 µg/g
  • PRX302 entry: IPSS ≥15, Qmax ≤12 mL/s, prostate 30–100 mL
  • PRX302 at 3 months: IPSS ↓~9, Qmax ↑3 mL/s; large placebo effect
  • NX-1207: pro-apoptotic; phase III negative → suspended

Classic traps

  • Trap: combination beats solifenacin 10 mg — Actually: BESIDE: only > solifenacin 5 mg
  • Trap: any antimuscarinic + mirabegron is proven — Actually: only solifenacin tested
  • Trap: antimuscarinic add-on to mirabegron untested — Actually: MILAI II, four agents
  • Trap: combination raises BP or QT — Actually: no; constipation is the added AE
  • Trap: vibegron combination established — Actually: phase IIb only
  • Trap: estradiol added to tolterodine helps — Actually: only tolterodine added after estradiol
  • Trap: onabotA weaker on anticholinergics — Actually: DIGNITY: equal
  • Trap: EMBARK compares anticholinergic users — Actually: all refractory
  • Trap: intraprostatic toxins treat BPH — Actually: = sham injection
  • Trap: PSD503 failed on efficacy — Actually: failed on acceptability

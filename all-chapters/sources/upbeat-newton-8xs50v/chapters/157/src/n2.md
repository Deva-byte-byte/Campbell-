### The numbers to lock in — Instalment 2

**Key facts, figures, thresholds**

- **Organ-confined disease by PSA**: ~80% <4 · ~66% 4–10 · <50% >10.
- **Nodal disease by PSA**: ~20% >20 · ~75% >50.
- **T1a ≤5% vs T1b >5%** of resected tissue; T1c needle-biopsy detected.
- **T2a ≤ half one side · T2b > half one side · T2c both sides**.
- **Grade groups**: 1 ≤6 · 2 = 3 + 4 · 3 = 4 + 3 · 4 = 8 · 5 = 9–10.
- **D'Amico**: low T1–2a + PSA ≤10 + Gleason ≤6 (83%); intermediate T2b or PSA >10–<20 or Gleason 7 (46%); high T2c or PSA >20 or Gleason 8–10 (29%) — 10-year freedom from disease after radical prostatectomy.
- **Oncotype DX 17 genes; Decipher 22 RNA biomarkers**; Prolaris cell-cycle progression.

**Gold standards / first-line**

- **Most important biopsy information**: histological grade; **most used system**: Gleason.
- **Pathological stage is more accurate than clinical stage**.
- **Staging imaging, high risk**: bone scan + CT or mpMRI; PSMA PET approved for initial high-risk staging.
- **Before repeat biopsy after a negative biopsy**: MRI.

**Eponyms and named systems**

- **Whitmore–Jewett**: historical staging system, replaced by TNM.
- **Gleason**: sum of two most prevalent patterns, 2–10.
- **D'Amico**: three-tier risk from T stage, PSA, Gleason.
- **CAPRA**: validated multiparameter risk score.

**Dates**

- **1975**: TNM adopted by the American Joint Committee on Cancer. **2005**: Gleason consensus adds tertiary pattern 5. **2017**: current TNM.

**When to operate / not operate**

- **PLND**: by nomogram-predicted nodal risk — staging, not therapeutic.
- **Seminal vesicle or capsular biopsy**: only for a large palpable basal tumour (selective view).

**Classic traps**

- **Seminal vesicle invasion = T3b, not T4.**
- **Non-regional nodes = M1a, not N1.**
- **Bilateral impalpable biopsy-detected cancer = T1c, not T2c.**
- **3 + 4 (group 2) ≠ 4 + 3 (group 3).**
- **D'Amico low risk needs all three criteria; higher groups need only one.**
- **No routine CT or bone scan** in asymptomatic low or intermediate risk.
- **High-grade tumours make less PSA per gram**, so PSA understates them.
- **Prostatic acid phosphatase is obsolete.**

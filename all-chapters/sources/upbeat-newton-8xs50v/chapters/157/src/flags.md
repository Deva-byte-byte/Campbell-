**Source errors and inconsistencies**, each carried in corrected form in the notes:

1. **"dividing PSA by PSAD"** (PSA Density paragraph) is circular. Correct: dividing PSA **by prostate volume** gives the PSAD.
2. **IsoPSA "total PSA ≥4 ng/m"** has a dropped letter. Correct: **ng/mL**.
3. **Table 157.1, PSA 4–10 row**: only "+" is printed in the DRE column, but the row has two value lines. By the table's pattern, the first line is DRE non-suspicious (–) and the second suspicious (+), and the table is rebuilt on that basis.
4. **Grade group 4** is printed as "Gleason 4 + 4 = 8". Grade group 4 is **any Gleason score 8 (4 + 4, 3 + 5, 5 + 3)**, which matches the chapter's own TNM paragraph ("8").
5. **USPSTF 2008** is given as "75 years of age or younger" versus "75 years or older", so the two bands overlap at 75. Correct: **younger than 75** versus **75 or older**.
6. **Trial names** differ between the text and the selected references. PLCO is "Prostate, Lung, Colon, and Ovary" in the text but "Prostate, Lung, Colorectal, and Ovarian" in the references. ERSPC is "European Randomized Trial of Prostate Cancer Screening" in the text but "European Randomised Study of Screening for Prostate Cancer" in the references. The notes use the reference-list (official) forms.
7. **MRI before biopsy**: the body text recommends MRI only before a *repeat* biopsy after a negative one. The key-points box recommends MRI **before prostate biopsy** in general. Both statements are carried.

**Extraction note.** The PDF text layer substitutes symbol-font glyphs: "!" for α, " for +, # for >, $ for <, ' for ≤, ( for =, & for ≥. The rendered pages are correct, and every value was read against the page images.

### The numbers to lock in — Instalment 1

**Key facts, figures, thresholds**

- **PSA biopsy thresholds**: 4 ng/mL (original FDA) · 2.5–3 (common) · 3 (ERSPC) · 10 over 70 years (AUA).
- **DRE adds little at PSA <3.0 ng/mL**.
- **PSAD ≥0.15** → biopsy at PSA 4–10 with normal DRE.
- **PSAV >0.75 ng/mL/yr** (PSA 4–10); **0.4** at lower PSA; **>0.35** 10–15 years before diagnosis → life-threatening cancer; **>2** in the year before diagnosis → cancer death after treatment.
- **%fPSA 25%** (PSA 4–10, benign DRE) → ~95% of cancers detected, ~20% of biopsies spared; ≈ PSAD 0.078.
- **5α-reductase inhibitor correction**: ×2 at ≥12 months; ×2.3 at 2 years; ×2.5 at 7 years; finasteride 1 mg = 5 mg effect.
- **Repeat PSA after 48 hours' abstinence** if minimally raised.
- **Table 157.1**: PSA 0–1 → ~9% yield; 4–10 → 17–32% (DRE –) vs 45–51% (DRE +); >10 → 43–65% vs 70–90%.
- **PSA era**: mortality down >40%; average age at diagnosis ~66.

**Commonest / first-line**

- **Most important influence on PSA**: prostate disease (cancer, BPH, prostatitis).
- **Most urologists combine PSA + DRE**; tPSA alone rarely triggers biopsy.

**Landmark trials**

- **ERSPC**: 20% relative reduction in cancer mortality at 16 years; invite 570 and diagnose 18 to prevent 1 death.
- **PLCO**: no mortality difference at 17 years — contamination, low power.
- **PCPT**: risk continuous with PSA; cancer present even at PSA 0–1.
- **Malmö Preventive Project**: PSA in the 40s predicts metastasis and death >25 years later.

**Dates and guidelines**

- **1993**: ERSPC and PLCO begin. **1994**: FDA approves PSA for early detection.
- **USPSTF**: 2008 against ≥75 · 2012 grade D · 2018 grade C for 55–69, against ≥70.
- **AUA** 55–69, 2-yearly; **NCCN** baseline at 45, repeat every 1–4 years; **American Cancer Society** from 50, earlier if high risk.

**Classic traps**

- **"Normal" PSA does not exclude cancer.**
- **Detection rate (per man screened) is not yield (per man biopsied).**
- **5α-reductase inhibitors lower tPSA but leave %fPSA unchanged**; both finasteride doses have the same effect.
- **DRE does not cause a false-positive PSA**; biopsy and transurethral resection raise PSA.
- **No antibiotics for a raised PSA alone.**
- **Hypogonadism lowers PSA** and can mask cancer.
- **PLCO's null result reflects contamination, not a lack of screening effect.**
- **fPSA is lower, not higher, in cancer**; bPSA marks BPH, proPSA marks cancer.
- **Changing assay** produces spurious PSA kinetics.

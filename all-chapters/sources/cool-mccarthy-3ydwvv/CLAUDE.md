# Campbell-Walsh-Wein chapter notes

This repository holds the standing instructions for producing chapter notes from
Campbell-Walsh-Wein Urology. Follow `STANDING_INSTRUCTIONS.md` in full for every
instalment summarised and every chapter compiled — it governs coverage, summary
style, tables, source flags, and the two delivered PDFs (Document 1 cheat sheet,
Document 2 chapter notes, both rendered with WeasyPrint in Carlito).

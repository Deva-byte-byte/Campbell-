# Chapter 40 — Clinical and Urodynamic Evaluation of Lower Urinary Tract Dysfunction in Children

<div class="box" markdown="1">
<p class="boxt">IF YOU READ NOTHING ELSE</p>

1. **Detrusor leak point pressure (DLPP) >40 cm H₂O** → upper-tract risk; <40 safe (McGuire 1981).
2. **Compliance (ΔV/ΔPdet): >20 normal; 10–20 moderate; <10 severe** (mL/cm H₂O).
3. **Expected bladder capacity (EBC): infant kg × 7; child (age + 2) × 30 mL.**
4. **Fill rate 5–10% EBC/min; stop at Pdet >40 or >150% EBC.**
5. **Urodynamics = gold standard**; only if the result changes management.
6. **Electromyography (EMG) lag time 2–6 s normal; <2 s = detrusor overactivity (DO); >6 s = bladder neck dysfunction.**
7. **Post-void residual (PVR) abnormal: 4–6 y >30 mL / >21%; 7–12 y >20 mL / >15%.**
8. **UMPIRE risk: <25 low; 25–39 or DO intermediate; ≥40 high.**
9. **Staccato never reaches zero; interrupted reaches zero** → EMG decides.
10. **Post-urodynamic urinary tract infection (UTI) 1.4%** → no universal culture or prophylaxis.

## CLINICAL EVALUATION

- **Secondary enuresis:** new wetting after **≥6 months dry**.
- **Sacral roots for bladder and rectum: S2–S4.**
- **Bristol target: type 3–4**, daily and painless; scale 1 (pellets) → 7 (watery).
- **Rome IV constipation:** age **≥4 y**, **≥2 of 6 criteria**, ≥1 month; **≤2 stools/week**, **≥1 soiling/week**.
- **ICCS constipation algorithm:** **rectum >30 mm → treat constipation**; wall **>6 mm empty / >3 mm full** → treat lower urinary tract symptoms (LUTS).
- **Both improve → maintain ≥3–6 months.**
- **Diaries:** **7-day bowel-bladder diary**; **48-hour frequency-volume chart**, mandatory if first-line therapy fails.
- **Questionnaires:**
    - **Dysfunctional Voiding Symptom Score (DVSS):** 10 items, max 30; **best for following treatment**.
    - **Dysfunctional Voiding and Incontinence Scoring System (DVISS):** 14 items, **cut-off 9**; **best for diagnosis**.
    - **Paediatric Urinary Incontinence Quality of Life Score (PinQ):** 20 items, max 80; quality of life; **does not track response**.
    - **Incontinence Symptom Index–Paediatric (ISI-P):** 11 items, max 44, **cut-off 9**; **self-report, age 11–17**.
- **Short Screening Instrument for Psychological Problems in Enuresis (SSIPPE):** **≥2 positive → full Child Behavior Checklist (CBCL)** → psychology referral.
- **Tri-ponderal mass index = kg/m³**, better than BMI in children; overweight **≥16.0 M / 16.8 F**.
- **Neurological level:** high lumbar = **hip flexion**; mid = **knee extension**; low = **dorsiflexion**; sacral = **plantar flexion**.
- **Absent anal wink or bulbocavernosus reflex** → S2–S4 arc interrupted.
- **Sacral dimple <2.5 cm from anus = low risk** → **spinal ultrasound ≤6 months**.
- **High-risk stigmata** (vascular lesion, lipoma, hair tuft, multiple, VACTERL) → **MRI**.
- **Spine ossifies 10–12 months** → ultrasound no longer adequate.
- **Labial fusion:** <2 y; resolves by puberty; **topical oestrogen, ~10% recur**.
- **Continuous wetting in a girl** → **ectopic ureteric orifice**.
- **Meatal stenosis:** circumcised boys; **treat only if the stream is deviated or narrowed**.

## LABORATORY TESTING

- **First test in LUTS: urinalysis**; a negative result is reassuring.
- **Culture only if symptomatic** (fever, pain, malaise, new wetting or spasm), especially on clean intermittent catheterisation (CIC).
- **Proteinuria → repeat**; persistent → **creatinine ± 24-hour protein**.
- **Blood tests** only if upper-tract disease is suspected.

## URODYNAMIC EVALUATION

- **ICCS terminology update: 2014.**
- **Uroflow:** voided volume, **maximum flow rate (Qmax)** and pattern matter most; **Qmax peak ≥2 s**.
- **Uroflow valid if** toilet-trained, **voided volume ≥50% EBC**, **≥2 curves**.
- **Patterns:**
    - **tower** = overactive bladder;
    - **staccato** = sphincter over-activity;
    - **interrupted** = underactive detrusor with Valsalva or detrusor-sphincter dyssynergia (DSD);
    - **plateau** = obstruction.
- **Wall thickness: >3 mm full, >5 mm empty.**
- **Bladder volume wall index (BVWI) normal 70–130%**; normal → better desmopressin response.
- **PVR valid only at 50–115% EBC, <5 min after voiding.**
- **Functional capacity normal ~70–115% EBC.**
- **Abnormal capacity (ICCS): <65% or >150% EBC.**
- **Catheter 6 Fr dual-lumen; EMG patches 3 and 9 o'clock; >1000 Hz sampling.**
- **Surface, not needle, EMG.**
- **No general anaesthesia; ketamine > midazolam** if sedation is essential.
- **EBC: newborn 30–50 mL → young adult 400–500 mL.**
- **Stop filling:** urge, pain, voiding, leak > fill, **Pdet >40**, **>150% EBC**.
- **Repeat fills: ICCS 2015 discretionary; first fill worst** → report conservatively.
- **Pdet = Pves − Pabd**, zeroed at the **pubic symphysis**.
- **Abdominal leak point pressure (ALPP), adult female:** **<60 intrinsic sphincter deficiency (ISD)**; **60–90 equivocal**; **>90 normal**.
- **Pressure-flow valid if** ≥50–100 mL voided, **≥50% functional capacity**.
- **Pdet at Qmax:** boys **66**, girls **57**; infants up to **118 (boys) / 75 (girls)**.
- **Danger signs:** poor compliance; DSD with poor emptying; sustained filling Pdet; **DLPP >40**; high voiding pressure with poor flow.
- **UMPIRE schedule:** baseline **3 months** → **1, 2, 3 y** → yearly to 5 if vesicoureteric reflux (VUR) or hostile bladder.
- **UMPIRE central vs local agreement ~50%.**

## SPECIFIC CLINICAL ENTITIES

- **European Bladder Dysfunction Study (2008):** urodynamics does not predict outcome in non-neurogenic LUTS.
- **Urodynamics if:** neurogenic or obstructive disease; **before reconstruction**; **refractory** non-neurogenic or cerebral palsy.
- **No urodynamics** as a first-line or routine test in non-neurogenic LUTS.
- **Myelomeningocele = 90% of open dysraphism.**
- **Sacral agenesis:** **~⅓ diagnosed late**; flat buttocks; **plain radiograph**; areflexia or DO + DSD.
- **Anorectal malformation → baseline urodynamics ~1 year after repair**, even with a normal cord.
- **Tethered cord:** conus fixed **below L2**.
- **Posterior urethral valves (PUV):** commonest male LUT obstruction; **~15% valve bladder**; DO → poor compliance → myogenic failure.
- **Sacrococcygeal teratoma resection → LUT dysfunction up to 50%.**
- **Cerebral palsy:** up to **⅓**; upper motor neuron; **DSD rare**.
- **Spinal cord injury:** shock → retention; **baseline video urodynamics 3–6 months**; higher lesion → more DSD.
- **Transverse myelitis:** **DO commonest**; motor recovery ≠ bladder recovery; **early CIC**.

<div class="traps" markdown="1">
<p class="boxt">CLASSIC TRAPS</p>

- **Trap:** high DLPP and low ALPP mean the same — **Actually:** high DLPP = upper-tract risk; low ALPP = ISD.
- **Trap:** interrupted curve = DSD — **Actually:** it may be Valsalva voiding; EMG decides.
- **Trap:** small bladder with compliance <20 is unsafe — **Actually:** judge by absolute Pdet <30–40.
- **Trap:** a bagged urine culture is reliable — **Actually:** reliable only if negative.
- **Trap:** a late or over-distended PVR is abnormal — **Actually:** it is invalid.
- **Trap:** hydronephrosis resolved, so compliance improved — **Actually:** compliance may not improve.
- **Trap:** DSD was dropped from UMPIRE because it is benign — **Actually:** it was dropped for poor agreement.
- **Trap:** neurological level predicts walking — **Actually:** level and mobility do not correlate.
- **Trap:** high infant voiding pressure = obstruction — **Actually:** up to 118 cm H₂O is physiological in infant boys.
- **Trap:** motor recovery in myelitis means bladder recovery — **Actually:** the bladder usually stays dysfunctional.
</div>

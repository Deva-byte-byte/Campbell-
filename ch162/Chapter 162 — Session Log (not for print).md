## Source-flag appendix

### Symbol substitution (text-layer extraction)
The PDF's text layer uses a systematic character substitution. Every value was checked against the page images, which print correctly. Key: `!` = ≥ (or ~ in "!20%" and "!60–70%"); `$` = >; `%` = <; `*` = ≤; `(` = +; `)` = =; `'` = ×; `&` = ±; `!/"` and `"/#` = α/β; `+m` = μm; `,` = − (minus); `#` in "nadir # 2" = +; `5"`-reductase = 5α-reductase.

### Source errors and inconsistencies (corrected in the documents)
1. **Orthovoltage and cobalt-60 depth of maximum dose** is given as "~1.25 cm" for both. Orthovoltage peaks at the skin surface and cobalt-60 at ~0.5 cm. Carried as "at or just below the skin surface".
2. **"Benjamin Stockwell Barrington"** should be **Barringer**.
3. **"Courtard"** vs "Coutard": inconsistent spelling; the correct spelling is Coutard.
4. **"18F-DCFFPyL"** should be **¹⁸F-DCFPyL**.
5. **"Patterson and Parker"** should be the **Paterson–Parker** rules.
6. **Photon energies are stated inconsistently**: ¹²⁵I is 28 keV in one place and 27 keV in another; ¹³¹Cs is 29 and 30 keV. Carried as ranges.
7. **Table 162.2 labels mismatch their descriptions**: "150% of prescription (V200)" and "115% of prescription (V150)". The subscripts do not match the percentages. The documents carry the percentage descriptions and drop the mislabelled subscripts.
8. **Oedema sentence** ("acute gland oedema can lead to 30% average prostate volume") is missing the word "increase".
9. **"37.5. Gy"** contains a stray full stop.
10. **Box 162.1**: nuclear grades 1–3 are not numbered in the source, although the 0–6 total implies a 0–3 scale. The descriptors are carried in ascending severity without inventing grade numbers.
11. **Level A heading "ROLE OF ADT ANDROGEN SUPPRESSION COMBINED WITH RADIATION"** duplicates itself. Rendered as "Role of Androgen Suppression Combined With Radiation".
12. **"this combines modality approach"** should be "combined".
13. **RTOG 0232 late toxicity**: the initial report is printed as grade ≥3 "12% vs 7%" immediately after "BT alone resulted in lower rates". The order is inverted, so BT alone = 7% and EBRT + BT = 12%. The two RTOG 0232 reports (initial and long-term) give different late-toxicity figures because they use different data cuts; both are tabulated.
14. **RTOG 0232 description** "103 patients with Gleason grade 3 (Gleason 7 [4+3])" uses Grade Group 3 terminology.
15. **Table 162.4 column order**: for PROG 95-09, the Dutch trial, MRC RT01, GETUG 06 and RTOG 0126, the outcome column lists the escalated-dose result first while the arms column lists standard dose first. The text confirms the escalated arm is better. Rebuilt with standard → escalated order.
16. **PROG 95-09 biochemical failure** is 32.4% vs 16.7% in the proton section and 32.0% vs 17.4% in the efficacy section (different data cut). Carried as ~32% vs ~17%.
17. **MD Anderson dose–toxicity sentence** says "5-year freedom from a complication above grade 2 was 26% (78 Gy) vs 12% (70 Gy)". These are complication rates, not freedom from complication. Corrected.
18. **Kuban 2010 moderate hypofractionation**: the text gives 5-year freedom from biochemical failure of 92% vs 92%, Table 162.5 gives 94% vs 97%. Both are non-significant; the table is condensed, so only the verdict is carried.
19. **Lukka/NCIC trial**: the text reports 53% vs 60% *failure* (worse with hypofractionation), while Table 162.5 labels the same figures "freedom from biochemical failure". The text version is carried.
20. **Pollack 2011** GU toxicity P = 0.028 in the text and 0.03 in the table (rounding).
21. **RTOG 86-10**: 456 patients in the text vs 471 in the table; "large (25 cm³)" should be a bulky tumour **≥25 cm²** (the symbol is lost). Carried as ≥25 cm².
22. **RTOG 92-02**: 1521 patients in the text vs 1554 in the table.
23. **RTOG 99-10**: 1489 patients in the text vs 1490 in the table; the text says "4 versus 8 months" and the table says "4 vs 9 months". The long arm was 36 weeks (~9 months). Carried as 4 vs 9 months.
24. **RADAR fractionation**: "66 Gy in 23 fractions" and "74 Gy in 27 fractions" are implausible fraction numbers (66 Gy/33 and 74 Gy/37 are intended). The documents carry total doses only.
25. **NCIC PR.3 overall survival CI** is printed "95% CI 90–78"; this should be 70–78.
26. **SPCG-7** "PSA of 70 ng/mL" has lost its symbol: PSA **<70**.
27. **MRC RT01 high-risk share**: 43% in the text vs 44% in the table.
28. **Buyyounouski**: the text calls 18 months "the cutoff point for time to nadir" but then analyses the *interval to biochemical failure*. The 18-month cut-off applies to the interval to biochemical failure.
29. **ORIOLE** "improved progression-free survival (19% vs 61%)": these are *progression* rates at 6 months. Corrected.
30. **Ultrahypofractionation dose per fraction** is stated three ways: 5–10 Gy (body), 6–10 Gy (EBRT key points) and 7–10 Gy (hypofractionation key points). The body definition (≥5 Gy) is used.
31. **Bounce frequency** is ~35% after LDR in the evaluation section and ~30% after BT in the outcomes section. Carried as "about a third".
32. **Table 162.10 (Dolezel 2015)**: late GI toxicity is 42.1% IMRT vs 20.5% 3D-CRT, reversing the pattern of every other series and the text's conclusion; possibly transposed columns. Noted in the notes as "the reverse in one series".
33. **Wallner urethral length** "20 mm + 11 mm": probably 20 ± 11 mm. Carried as "about 20 ± 11 mm".
34. **PSMAddition** is described as "non-mCRPC patients". The trial enrols metastatic hormone-sensitive disease; corrected.
35. **Huggins and Hodges** is cited as 1972 (the reprint); the discovery was 1941. The date 1941 is carried.

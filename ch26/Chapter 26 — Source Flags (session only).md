## Character substitution key (systematic, and not one-to-one)

| Glyph in source | Usual meaning | Exceptions resolved by context |
|---|---|---|
| `!` | > or ≥ | Sometimes "about": ">80% of nosocomial UTIs" was carried as "about 80%"; *S. saprophyticus* "!10%" as "about 10%"; "!50% had an abnormality" as "about half". |
| `"` | < | Also used as ≥ in some places: "`"`10⁵ CFU/mL remains the standard" means ≥10⁵; "`"`100 CFU/mL" in catheterised patients means ≥10². |
| `#` | ≥ | Used as ≤ in Thorley's criteria: pyelonephritis symptomatic for ≤5 days before admission. Also the dropped "fl" ligature ("re#ux", "cipro#oxacin", "#ora"). |
| `)` | ≤ | "≤10 mm", "≤6 months", "≤14 days", "≤24 h". |
| `%` | α | α3β1 integrin, α1-antitrypsin. It is ≤ in "short-term (≤7 days)" methenamine therapy. |
| `$` | β | β-lactam, β-lactamase. |
| `&` | ✓ (culture condition) in Table 26.1 | + in "Na+". |
| `'` | = | "n = 303". |
| `*` | × | "23 × 12 cm", "×640". |
| Lost superscripts | — | "105/102/104" = 10⁵/10²/10⁴; "99mTc" = ⁹⁹ᵐTc. |

**Places where one glyph is used for opposite directions in the same passage.** These were resolved by clinical sense and the chapter's other statements:

- **Renal abscess, "`"`5 cm":**
    - In the Lee series ("abscesses `"`5 cm resolved on antibiotics"), it means ≤5 cm.
    - In "percutaneous drainage first line for most renal abscesses `"`5 cm", it means >5 cm.
- **Perinephric abscess:**
    - "`"`3 cm" means <3 cm (antibiotics alone).
    - "!3 cm" means >3 cm (drain).
- **Table 26.19 orthopaedic hardware:**
    - "!2 yr ago" means >2 years (no prophylaxis).
    - "`"`2 yr ago" means <2 years (prophylaxis).
- **SUFU urodynamics:** "age `"`70" means >70. `"` normally means <, but "age <70" as a risk factor makes no sense.
- **Transrectal biopsy:** "E. coli has `"`20% fluoroquinolone resistance" means >20%.
- **Catheter dipstick:** "#3 days" was read as a short-term catheter of about ≤3 days.
- **SCI:** "catheterisation `"`10 years" means >10 years.

## Source errors and internal inconsistencies (corrected versions are the ones taught)

1. **IDSA 2019 and the elderly (wrong).** The Elderly Management section says the latest IDSA guideline "recommends screening for or treating ASB in older, community-dwelling people who are functionally impaired or … in long-term care facilities". That is the reverse of IDSA 2019, and it contradicts the chapter's own ASB and Elderly statements. **Corrected: IDSA recommends *against* screening or treating.**
2. **Aminoglycoside nomogram units.** "7 mg/mL at 10 hours" should read **7 mg/L (µg/mL)**. The figure's axis is in µg/mL.
3. **Fig. 26.10 legend versus figure.** The legend lists ampicillin–sulbactam as resistant. The printed report shows **Intermediate**. The notes give both.
4. **Nitrofurantoin renal cut-off, three values:**
    - Text: historically 60 mL/min, then **30 mL/min** after Beers 2015.
    - Table 26.6: **<50 mL/min**, from an older formulary source.
    - All three are tabulated with their sources.
5. **ASB prevalence, text versus Table 26.2:**
    - Pregnancy: 2–7% versus 1.9–9.5%.
    - Community men over 75: 6–15% versus 3.6–19%.
    - Long-term care men: 15–40% versus 14–50%.
    - The source series differ, and both are tabulated.
6. **Catheter bacteriuria per day.** The CAUTI section gives about 10% per day; the catheterisation section gives 5–10% per day. Both are stated.
7. **AUA BPS wording.** Single-dose "first- or second-generation cephalosporins or TMP-SMX covering enterococci" contradicts Table 26.5, where cephalosporins never cover enterococci. The notes keep the statement, and the traps teach that cephalosporins do not cover enterococci.
8. **Wound class of bowel procedures.** Table 26.18 puts "use of bowel segments" in Class III (contaminated). Table 26.17 labels small- and large-bowel procedures clean-contaminated. The notes flag this in the post-table trap.
9. **"Skin flora (coagulase-negative staphylococci and gram-negative bacilli)."** Gram-negative bacilli are not skin flora. The notes say only "cover skin flora".
10. **Fig. 26.25 versus AFBN text.** The text calls the AFBN lesion "relatively sonolucent". The legend describes "slightly higher echo texture". Both are noted.
11. **Dipstick accuracy in the elderly.** Specificity is given as 71% with a 95% confidence interval of 55–71%: the upper bound equals the point estimate. The numbers are omitted anyway under the study-data rule.
12. **Table 26.4, vancomycin.** "Inhibition of cell wall synthesis (at β-lactams)" is garbled. It is carried as "at a different point from β-lactams", matching the resistance column.
13. **Table 26.17 footnote "ID, identification".** In context this means infectious disease consultation. Corrected.
14. **ST131 citation anachronism.** The fluoroquinolone-resistance hallmark of ST131 is cited to a 1983 paper that predates ST131. The citation is not carried.
15. **Global Prevalence Study dates.** The study period "2005–2017" is cited to a 2014 publication. The date is not carried.
16. **"Bib_598 et al. (2005)".** An unresolved citation placeholder in the Vaginal Surgery section. The citation is not carried.
17. **Typos and names:**
    - "E. faecali" is *E. faecalis*.
    - "M. hominus" is *M. hominis*.
    - "prostrate" (Table 26.16) is prostate.
    - "Uro-Vaxum" is Uro-Vaxom (OM-89).
    - Fig. 26.9's citation "3:616e48" is garbled.

Chapter 157 — Diagnosis and Staging of Prostate Cancer — Numbers, Gold Standards and Traps

If you read nothing else

  • ERSPC (European Randomised Study of Screening for Prostate Cancer): 20% ↓ cancer mortality, 16 yr; 570 invited + 18 extra diagnoses per death prevented
  • PLCO (Prostate, Lung, Colorectal, Ovarian): no mortality difference — contamination, low power
  • USPSTF (US Preventive Services Task Force) 2018: grade C, 55–69, shared decision; none ≥70
  • Prostate disease = main PSA (prostate-specific antigen) determinant; organ- not cancer-specific
  • 5α-reductase inhibitor (5ARI) ≥12 mo → PSA × 2 (× 2.3 at 2 yr, × 2.5 at 7 yr)
  • Repeat raised PSA before biopsy; no empirical antibiotics
  • PSA density (PSAD) ≥0.15; velocity (PSAV) >0.75 ng/mL/yr; % free PSA (%fPSA) ≤25% — all at PSA 4–10
  • Gleason 3+4 = grade group (GG) 2; 4+3 = GG3 — primary pattern decides
  • D'Amico low = T1–2a + PSA ≤10 + Gleason ≤6 (all three)
  • Low/intermediate risk → no routine CT or bone scan; high → bone scan + CT/mpMRI

Diagnosis

Screening

  • Since PSA era: mortality ↓ >40%; advanced stage at diagnosis ↓ ~75%
  • Highest evidence: randomised screened-vs-unscreened trials
  • ERSPC, PLCO: both began 1993; endpoint cancer-specific mortality
  • Overtreatment worst >65; mean age at diagnosis ~66
  • USPSTF: 2008 insufficient ≤75, against ≥75 → 2012 grade D → 2018 grade C
  • ACS (American Cancer Society): from 50 if life expectancy ≥10 yr; earlier if Black or first-degree relative diagnosed <65
  • AUA (American Urological Association): shared decision 55–69; 2-yearly interval
  • NCCN (National Comprehensive Cancer Network): baseline PSA at 45; retest 1–4 yr
  • ACPM (American College of Preventive Medicine): insufficient evidence for population screening

Digital rectal examination (DRE)

  • Limited value at PSA <3.0 ng/mL; fair reproducibility only
  • DRE barely moves PSA — within assay error
  • Standard practice: PSA + DRE together

PSA

  • Kallikrein family; free + complexed (α1-antichymotrypsin) = total (tPSA)
  • FDA (Food and Drug Administration) approval for early detection: 1994
  • +4% PSA per mL prostate volume; volume ~30%, age ~5% of variance
  • Lower PSA: hypogonadism, obesity, statins, 5ARI, BPH (benign prostatic hyperplasia) surgery
  • Finasteride 1 mg = 5 mg for PSA lowering
  • 5ARI: baseline PSA first; or use nadir as new baseline
  • Ejaculation equivocal → repeat after 48 h abstinence
  • Black people: higher PSA without cancer
  • Malmö: PSA in 40s predicts metastasis/death >25 yr later
  • PCPT (Prostate Cancer Prevention Trial): risk continuous — no safe PSA

Biopsy thresholds

  • 4 ng/mL original limit; 2.5–3 commonly used; ERSPC 3
  • >70 yr: higher cut-off, e.g. 10 ng/mL
  • Table 157.1: PSA 0–1 → ~9% biopsy yield; >10 + suspicious DRE → ~50% high-grade

Derivatives and markers

  • %fPSA 25% ≈ PSAD 0.078 specificity — no volume needed
  • %fPSA unaffected by race or 5ARI
  • cPSA (complexed): specificity > tPSA, ≈ %fPSA
  • PSAV lethal risk: >0.35 ng/mL/yr 10–15 yr pre-diagnosis (~5×); >2 ng/mL/yr in year pre-diagnosis
  • PSAV lower threshold: 0.4 ng/mL/yr at low tPSA
  • bPSA = BPH marker; proPSA ↑ in cancer; hK2 (human kallikrein 2) tracks grade/volume
  • phi (Prostate Health Index) = tPSA + fPSA + [−2]proPSA; FDA-approved
  • 4Kscore = tPSA + fPSA + intact PSA + human kallikrein 2
  • Urine: SelectMDx (HOXC6, DLX1); ExoDx (3-gene exosomal); PCA3; TMPRSS2:ERG; MPS = tPSA + PCA3 + TMPRSS2:ERG
  • IsoPSA (blood): validated ≥50 yr, PSA ≥4
  • Repeat-biopsy decision: PCA3; ConfirmMDx (tissue methylation; negative predictive value >90%)
  • tPSA alone rarely triggers biopsy

Staging

Stage

  • Pathological stage more accurate than clinical; survival inversely related
  • Post-RP (radical prostatectomy) predictors: grade, margins, extracapsular extension, SVI (seminal vesicle invasion), nodes
  • TNM (tumour, node, metastasis): American Joint Committee on Cancer 1975; Whitmore–Jewett historical
  • T1a ≤5%, T1b >5% of resected tissue; T1c = needle biopsy, impalpable
  • T2a ≤½ one side; T2b >½ one side; T2c both
  • T3a extraprostatic; T3b SVI; T4 fixed/other adjacent structures
  • M1a non-regional nodes; M1b bone; M1c other ± bone
  • Prognostic groups: TNM + PSA + grade group

Predicting extent

  • Organ-confined: ~80% PSA <4; 66% 4–10; <50% >10
  • Nodes: ~20% PSA >20; 75% >50
  • Poorly differentiated → less PSA per gram
  • Prostatic acid phosphatase obsolete
  • Abnormal DRE → ↑ grade group 4–5

Grade

  • Gold standard grading: Gleason (1966); grade = most important biopsy finding
  • Score = primary + secondary pattern, 2–10; 2–5 no longer assigned
  • Tertiary 5 on biopsy: 3+4 → 3+5; 4+3 → 4+5
  • GG1 ≤6; GG2 3+4; GG3 4+3; GG4 8; GG5 9–10
  • Adverse biopsy features: intraductal, cribriform, SVI, periprostatic fat
  • Seminal vesicle biopsy: only for large palpable basal tumour

Risk groups

  • D'Amico intermediate: T2b or PSA >10–20 or Gleason 7
  • D'Amico high: T2c or PSA >20 or Gleason 8–10
  • 10-yr disease-free after RP: 83% / 46% / 29%
  • Also validated: CAPRA (Cancer of the Prostate Risk Assessment)

Imaging

  • mpMRI (multiparametric MRI) = T2 + diffusion-weighted + dynamic contrast
  • Prior negative biopsy → strongly consider MRI; suspicious lesion → targeted biopsy
  • PSMA PET (prostate-specific membrane antigen): 68Ga PSMA-11, 18F-DCFPyL — FDA-approved, initial staging, high risk
  • Staging imaging overused in screened populations

Molecular staging and PLND

  • Prolaris cell cycle; Oncotype DX 17 genes; Decipher 22 RNAs
  • PLND (pelvic lymph node dissection): no consistent oncological benefit — staging only
  • Operate (PLND) if: nomogram-predicted nodal risk exceeds guideline threshold

Classic traps

  • Trap: PLCO proves screening useless — Actually: contaminated control arm, underpowered
  • Trap: USPSTF says grade D — Actually: grade C since 2018 (55–69)
  • Trap: PSA <4 excludes cancer — Actually: risk continuous; ~9% yield at 0–1
  • Trap: Raw PSA on 5ARI — Actually: double it (≥12 mo)
  • Trap: Low PSA in hypogonadism reassures — Actually: may mask cancer
  • Trap: Antibiotics for raised PSA — Actually: not without urinary symptoms
  • Trap: Gleason 7 is one group — Actually: 3+4 = GG2, 4+3 = GG3
  • Trap: SVI is T4 — Actually: T3b
  • Trap: Non-regional nodes are N1 — Actually: M1a
  • Trap: Bone scan for all new cancers — Actually: high risk only

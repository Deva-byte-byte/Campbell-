Chapter 25 — Molecular Genetics and Cancer Biology — Numbers, Gold Standards and Traps

If you read nothing else

  • TP53: commonest mutated gene in cancer; mutated in >50% of malignancies.
  • RB1 phosphorylation by cyclin D–CDK4/6 + E–CDK2 → E2F free → S phase.
  • Two hits (Knudson) — exceptions: dominant negative (TP53), haploinsufficiency (p27).
  • GSTP1 hypermethylation: commonest somatic change in prostate cancer (>90%).
  • TMPRSS2–ERG: ~half of prostate cancers; commonest fusion.
  • BRCA1/2 loss → PARP inhibitor (approved mPC); ATM, CDK12 → little benefit.
  • MMR deficiency/MSI → immunotherapy (tissue-agnostic).
  • VHL loss → HIF pseudohypoxia; ccRCC 3p loss near-universal; belzutifan = HIF-2α inhibitor.
  • FGFR: 15–20% advanced bladder UC, up to 37% UTUC → erdafitinib; test all advanced UC.
  • Predictive marker needs biomarker–treatment interaction; prognostic = parallel lines.

Basic Molecular Genetics

  • RNA polymerase II makes pre-mRNA; cap, poly-A, splice.
  • AR splice variants lack ligand-binding domain → castration resistance.
  • Start codon AUG (methionine); three stop codons.
  • rRNA ~80% of cellular RNA; tRNA 75–85 bases.
  • Initiation rate-limiting; elongation fastest.

Somatic and Germline Testing

  • Somatic: tumour tissue → targeted therapy. Germline: blood/saliva → risk, family.

Inactivation of Tumour Suppressor Genes and Activation of Oncogenes

  • Point mutations: silent, missense, nonsense (premature stop).
  • Indel → frameshift.
  • Suppressor loss routes (4): homozygous deletion; LOH + mutation; two mutations; LOH + methylation.
  • Oncogene activation (3): mutation, amplification, rearrangement.
  • MET: germline activating → hereditary papillary RCC.
  • Isochromosome: one arm lost, other duplicated; ring = intrachromosomal fusion; dicentric = end-to-end.

Cell Cycle Deregulation

  • Cycle ~24 h; G0 = reversible quiescence (default).
  • Cyclin order: D → E → A → B; 12 mammalian cyclins.
  • Cyclin B–CDK1 drives mitosis (lamin breakdown).
  • G1/S: external signals + damage; G2/M: damage only.
  • S-phase arrest: ATM → Cdc25A destroyed → CDK2 off.
  • G2/M: ATM → CHEK1/2 → Cdc25 sequestered by 14-3-3σ.
  • TP53 mediates both G1/S and G2/M arrest.
  • Spindle checkpoint blocks anaphase-promoting complex.
  • Aneuploidy: near-universal in solid tumours.
  • ↓ MAD1/MAD2 in RCC; ↓ MAD2 testis; hBUBR1 mutations rare.
  • Cip/Kip (p21, p27, p57) broad; INK4 (p15, p16, p18, p19) CDK4/6 only.
  • p21 ↑ after DNA damage; p27 high in quiescence.
  • RB1 phosphorylated at eight sites; E2F + DP1–3.

DNA Damage and Repair

  • Replication error ~3 bases per division; genome >3 × 109 bases.
  • Oxidative lesions ~10 000 per cell per day.
  • NER: bulky/UV; BER: oxidative/deamination (OGG1, MUTYH).
  • MMR loss → ~100-fold ↑ mutation rate; MLH1.
  • HR: precise, sister template, S/G2 only; BRCA1/2, RAD51.
  • NHEJ: error-prone; Ku70/80, DNA-PKcs, ligase IV/XRCC4.
  • γH2AX spreads up to 30 Mbp from break.
  • Loeb: mutator phenotype explains excess mutations.

Apoptosis and Cell Death

  • Initiator caspases 8, 9, 10; executioner 3, 6, 7; ≥13 total.
  • Intrinsic: cytochrome c + Apaf-1 = apoptosome → caspase-9.
  • Extrinsic: FasL–CD95 → FADD → caspase-8; FLIP inhibits.
  • Bax transactivated by TP53; Bcl-2 binds Bax.
  • Bid (cut by caspase-8) links extrinsic → intrinsic.
  • Telomere loss ~100 bp per division.
  • Telomerase high in 85–90% of tumours; ALT via ATRX/DAXX.
  • Short telomeres predict prostate cancer recurrence.
  • SA-β-gal marks senescence; SASP double-edged.
  • ADT, enzalutamide → therapy-induced senescence.
  • Autophagy: mTOR/AMPK → ULK1/2 → Beclin-1; chloroquine inhibits.
  • CpG islands ~40% of promoters; tumour loss 20–50% methylcytosine.
  • RASSF1A methylated up to 97% bladder; p16 27–60% UC.
  • LINE-1 hypomethylation bladder cancer.
  • DNMT1 maintenance; DNMT3A/3B de novo.
  • Activating H3K4me3; repressive H3K9me3, H3K27me3 (EZH2).
  • EZH2 high → PSA recurrence; ↑ in CRPC.
  • lncRNA >200 nt (HOTAIR, MALAT1); miRNA 18–25 nt; piRNA 24–31 nt.
  • ~98% genome transcribed as ncRNA; <2% protein-coding.
  • VHL inactivated ~60% RCC (mutation ~50%, methylation ~10%).
  • Warburg (Nobel 1931): aerobic glycolysis; CA9, MCT4, NHE1.
  • Vasculogenic mimicry: tumour cells line vessels.

Next-Generation Sequencing Techniques

  • Bisulfite: unmethylated C → U; methylated unchanged.
  • ATAC-seq = open chromatin; ChIP-seq = protein–DNA binding.
  • scRNA-seq: barcode cells before library.

Advances in the Understanding of Molecular Genetics in Genitourinary Tumours

  • AVPC: loss of ≥2 of TP53, RB1, PTEN; overlaps NEPC.
  • SPOP: commonest mutated suppressor, primary prostate cancer.
  • MYC on 8q; 8q24 germline risk locus.
  • AR amplification (incl. enhancer, ecDNA) → ADT resistance.
  • ccRCC 75–80%; papillary 15–20%.
  • VHL 60–70% mutated + 5–10% silenced in ccRCC.
  • PBRM1 ~40% (better); BAP1, SETD2 ~10% (worse).
  • VHL syndrome → >70% lifetime RCC.
  • Chromophobe: distal nephron; mitochondrial DNA, mTOR.
  • Translocation RCC: MiT/TFE; young women.
  • Sarcomatoid: PD-L1+, immunotherapy-responsive.
  • UC site: 90% bladder, 10% upper tract.
  • Consensus MIBC: six subtypes; basal/squamous 35% largest; NE-like 3%.
  • HER2 9–12% MIBC; no approved agent.
  • ERCC2 10–18% → cisplatin sensitivity; ~10% germline DDR.
  • TGCT: 12p gain; KIT/KRAS/NRAS; TP53 rare.
  • miR-371a-3p: >90% sensitivity and specificity.
  • HPRC MET → papillary type 1; HLRCC FH → type 2, collecting duct.
  • BHD (FLCN) → hybrid oncocytic, chromophobe; HP-JT HRPT2.
  • HOXB13, BRCA2, Lynch → prostate; Lynch → urothelial.

General Principles of Clinical NGS Assays

  • VAF = allele fraction × purity: 0.5 × 0.6 = 30%.
  • VAF ~50% → heterozygous germline; ~100% → homozygous germline.
  • High VAF = clonal; low = subclonal.
  • WES cuts cost ~2 orders vs WGS.
  • CHIP mimics tumour variants at single-digit VAF in cfDNA.
  • MRD: WES primary → bespoke panel → ultra-deep ctDNA.
  • AR-V7 on CTCs; EpCAM enrichment.

Clinical Application of Molecular Biomarkers in Genitourinary Cancers

  • Short-term ADT: unfavourable intermediate risk only; long-term: high risk.
  • ARR = baseline risk × RRR: 30% × 50% = 15%; 3% → 1.5%.
  • PORTOS predicts PORT dose-escalation benefit (SAKK 08/09).
  • HSD3B1 1245A>C → ADT-resistant, abiraterone-sensitive.

Classic traps

  • Trap: every suppressor needs two hits — Actually: TP53 dominant negative; p27 haploinsufficient.
  • Trap: G2/M senses growth factors — Actually: DNA damage only.
  • Trap: NHEJ is precise — Actually: error-prone; HR precise (S/G2).
  • Trap: extrinsic apoptosis needs TP53 — Actually: TP53-independent.
  • Trap: ATM loss = BRCA for PARP — Actually: little benefit.
  • Trap: TP53 common in testis — Actually: rare.
  • Trap: PBRM1 = poor prognosis — Actually: better; BAP1/SETD2 worse.
  • Trap: siRNA broad — Actually: one target; miRNA many.
  • Trap: VAF 50% = somatic clonal — Actually: suspect germline without matched normal.
  • Trap: prognostic = predictive — Actually: needs treatment interaction.

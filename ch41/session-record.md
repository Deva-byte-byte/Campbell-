## The numbers to lock in (whole chapter, by instalment block)

**Instalments 1–2.**
- **Embryology**: bud at weeks 4–6.
- **Paquin**: 5:1 vs 1.4:1.
- **Prevalence**: VUR about 30% with UTI vs 17% other indications.
- **Sex**: infant M:F up to 3:1 → 1:1 by 2 years; uncircumcised infants 10–12× UTI; IRSC male entrants 10% (US) vs 24% (Europe), with circumcision 62% vs 5%.
- **Table 41.2**: 38.6 / 26.6 / 19.7 / 7.6%.
- **Fetus**: prenatal hydronephrosis 1–5%.
- **Siblings**: about 30% (to 50%), higher under 3.
- **AUA sibling VCUG triggers**: cortical abnormality, asymmetry, UTI, hydronephrosis grade 3–4.
- **Grading**: international (1981), five grades; RNC three grades.
- **UDR**: false-pelvis ureter ÷ L1–L3.
- **Aetiology**: spina bifida VUR up to 30%; normal voiding pressure about 100 cm H₂O.
- **Traps**: grade is a snapshot; reflux during filling = poor prognosis; primary and secondary VUR coexist.

**Instalments 3–4.**
- **Imaging**: VCUG ≤70 cm H₂O; RNC about 1% of the continuous-fluoroscopy dose and misses grade I; cyclic study +12–20%; indirect RNC sensitivity 70–90%; PIC from 1 m.
- **DMSA**: 98% sensitivity, 92% specificity; VUR about 3× cortical defects; VUR in 30% of pyelonephritis.
- **Circumcision**: NNT 111 vs 4.
- **BBD**: Swedish dysfunction 20% → 34%; RIVUR+CUTIE recurrent UTI about half (BBD+VUR) vs a fifth (VUR) vs a third (BBD).
- **Gold standards**: VCUG/direct RNC; DMSA.
- **Traps**: ultrasound cannot diagnose VUR; routine cystoscopy is useless; UVJ obstruction signs are a relative contraindication to injection.

**Instalment 5.**
- **Thresholds**: highest scarring risk in the first year, and under 4 after one UTI; intrarenal reflux at 2 mm Hg in neonates.
- **Hypertension**: 34–38% with scars.
- **Renal failure**: USRDS 1.9 → 0.6 per million.
- **AAP**: 2–24 months; 50,000 CFU/mL.
- **NICE**: under 6 months, 6 months–3 years and over 3 years rules; 48-hour response.
- **TDA**: DMSA first; follow-up DMSA at 4–6 months; normal DMSA → <1% high-grade VUR; about 70% of febrile UTIs abnormal.
- **Eponyms**: Ransley–Risdon (sterile VUR does not scar; "big bang"); Hodson (obstruction plus VUR scars without infection); Smellie (paradigm shift).
- **Traps**: correcting VUR does not cure hypertension; scar vs dysplasia.

**Instalment 6.**
- **UPJO**: in VUR 0.75–3.6%, high grade 5×; VUR in UPJO up to about 5–9%; four VCUG signs; fix obstruction first.
- **Duplex**: lower pole (Weigert–Meyer).
- **Renal anomalies**: MCDK contralateral VUR about 25%; agenesis contralateral VUR 24–28%.
- **Megacystis-megaureter**: non-obstructive; never operate on the outlet.
- **Adulthood**: Dx/HA calcifies in >50%.
- **Pregnancy**: pyelonephritis about 33% vs <5%; scars → hypertension about 3×, pre-eclampsia about 8×; offspring VUR 43%.
- **Traps**: a ureter entering a diverticulum will not resolve; obstructive refluxing megaureter is under-graded.

**Instalment 7.**
- **Resolution**: grade II 63–85%, III about 50% (90% within 4 years), IV–V few; observation 5 years.
- **Landmark trials**: IRSC (UTI 38% both arms; scars equal); Birmingham (scars equal, all within 2 years); Swedish (girls 19/23/57%; boys no difference); RIVUR (607 children; 23.6 → 12.9%; scarring 11.9 vs 10.2%; NNT 5 vs 18); PRIVENT (absolute reduction 6%).
- **Open correction**: >95%.
- **Recurrence**: 15% recurrent febrile UTI within 2 years, 15% of those scar; BBD 45% vs 15%; off CAP 8–10%.
- **Hidas 2-year BUTI**: 8.6 / 26 / 62.5%.
- **Agents**: under 2 months, trimethoprim or amoxicillin; no sulfonamide.
- **When to operate**: breakthrough febrile UTI on CAP despite good habits.
- **Traps**: CAP prevents UTI, not scars; Smellie's sensitive-vs-resistant BUTI logic.

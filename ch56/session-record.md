## The numbers to lock in

### Instalment A
- **Proximal repair**: 25–75% need additional procedures after a planned two-stage repair; distal repair is effectively successful in about 80%.
- **Postoperative care**: diversion 3–21 days; dressing 72 h (authors); analgesia tapered by 48–72 h.
- **Table 56.1**: curvature and torsion >30°; stenosis <8 Fr prepubertal / <12 Fr postpubertal; glans fusion <2 mm; skin surplus ≥2 cm.
- **Fistula**: just under 10%; repair at ≥6–12 months; recurrence about 20%; diversion 7–14 days for complex repairs.
- **Meatus**: ≥8 Fr postoperatively.
- **Eponyms**: Poiseuille (resistance ∝ length, ∝ 1/r⁴); Duckett ("hypospadiology"); Thiersch–Duplay; Byars flaps; modified Ulaanbaatar; Cecil modification.
- **When not to operate**: glans dehiscence without voiding symptoms; small fistula in the immediate postoperative period (wait for tissue to soften).
- **Traps**: a coronal fistula needs revision, not simple excision; Foley lateral traction causes dehiscence; routine dilatation is unnecessary; calibration without symptoms is not stenosis.

### Instalment B
- **Thresholds**: stricture <1 cm still does poorly with incision; BXO recurrence up to 40%.
- **Pfistermuller TIP meta-analysis**: 10.6% complications, 4.5% reoperation; fistula 5.7%, meatal stenosis 3.6%, stricture 1.3%.
- **Outcome rates**: distal success 83–95%; proximal complications 23–68%; proximal fistula 3–45%; recurrent curvature 0–10%; STAG <25%; Byars 30–70%; recurrent UTI about 2%.
- **Timing**: only about 50% of complications appear in year 1; fistula early, stricture late.
- **HOPE**: 1–10 per item; 0–30° = 10, >70° = 1.
- **Commonest**: fistula.
- **Traps**: a tourniquet hides basal curvature; BXO invades buccal mucosa; proximal TIP is no longer recommended; TIP causes more meatal stenosis than Mathieu.

### Instalment C
- **Thresholds**: buccal graft 10% oversize; fenestrate 18–21 G; wait ≥6 (up to 12) months; massage from 2 weeks; steroid from 1 month; buccal staged success 30–75%; bladder mucosa prolapse up to 38%; tissue expander sufficient in 95%, complications 41%; nitroglycerin 24 h; Cecil release 6–8 weeks vs modified 9–12 months.
- **Gold standard**: buccal mucosa; lip for the glans.
- **Eponyms**: Devine (no "small operations"); Cecil (1946); Culp (1951); SOS flap.
- **Traps**: single-stage full-thickness skin graft shrinks; bladder mucosa contracts in air; closing the buccal donor site adds pain without benefit.

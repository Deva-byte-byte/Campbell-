## The numbers to lock in

### Instalments 1–3 (new)
- **Incidence and spectrum**: 1 in 150–300; distal 70–85%, proximal 10–25%; MIP 5%; incomplete foreskin 95%.
- **Embryology**: anal and genital separation at 7 weeks; window weeks 8–16; SRY at 7–8 weeks; DHT at 8–12 weeks; ventral preputial fusion at about 20 weeks.
- **GMS score**: each component 1–4; shaft <30 / 30–60 / >60°.
- **Associations**: hernia or hydrocele 9–16%; cryptorchidism about 7% (about 10% proximal). DSD with non-palpable testis about 50% vs 15%; proximal vs distal 64% vs 7%; severe hypospadias counted as 46,XY DSD since 2006.
- **Aetiology**: twin concordance 9–27%; first-degree relative 9–15%; chromosomal abnormality up to 30%; about 200 syndromes; incidence rise up to 3% per year (disputed).
- **Timing**: repair at 6–12 months (AAP 1996) vs AAP range 6–18 months (see flag 8); genital awareness about 18 months; review by 18 months.
- **Androgens**: glans <14 mm (risk) vs <15 mm (authors' threshold for testosterone); testosterone cypionate 2 mg/kg IM at 5 and 2 weeks; +4 mm (two doses) / +2 mm (one); side effects resolve within 6 months; ketorolac 0.5 mg/kg.
- **Curvature**: grading <30 / 30–45 / ≥45°. Algorithm: <30° plication; >30° divide plate; >30° lengthen. Authors plicate ≤15°. Dermal graft 20% oversize; corporotomy from 9 to 3 o'clock; recurrence 28% (plication) vs 9% (graft); congenital curvature recurrence 2–15%. Adults bothered by 20–30°.
- **Eponyms**: Duckett; Glenister (ectodermal theory); Kurzrock (two-zipper); Nesbit (1965, excisional plication); Baskin (12 o'clock nerve-free zone); Devine and Horton (1973 staging); Gittes–McLaughlin (saline erection).
- **Traps**: meatal position underestimates severity; never circumcise MIP or curvature unassessed; a normal karyotype does not exclude DSD; tourniquet hides basal curvature; not dividing the plate predicts recurrence.

### Instalments 4–6 (new)
- **Technique**: sutures 6-0 to 8-0 in infants (5-0 in adults); tourniquet released every 30 min; dressing 72 h.
- **Repairs**: TIP >90% of distal and about 80% of midshaft repairs; TD sized to 12 Fr; TIP width 12 mm; TIP stops 2–3 mm short; plate plus flap ≤12 mm (OIF).
- **Staging**: interval ≥6 months (authors 8–12); graft massage from 2–4 weeks; 25–75% need additional procedures.
- **Eponyms and dates**: MAGPI (Duckett 1981, glanular only); MIV (Decter 1991); GAP (Zaontz 1989); pyramid; TD (1869/1874); TIP (Snodgrass 1994; Rich 1989 hinge); Mathieu/SLAM; OIF; TPIF (Duckett 1980); Bracka; STAG; STAC; Byars (1951); Ulaanbaatar; Belman flap (1988).
- **Abandoned or not to do**: proximal TIP (stricture, recurrent curvature); hair-bearing skin; MAGPI beyond the glans.
- **Traps**: MAGPI on a coronal meatus retracts; an over-wide OIF flap forms a diverticulum; TIP incision contraction causes stenosis; a graft over corporotomies contracts (hence STAC).

### Instalments 7–9 (as in the earlier partial record)
- Fistula just under 10% (repair ≥6–12 months; recurrence about 20%); meatal stenosis definitions; >30° curvature or torsion; glans fusion <2 mm; skin surplus ≥2 cm; BXO recurrence up to 40%.
- Pfistermuller TIP meta-analysis (10.6% / 4.5%; fistula 5.7% > stenosis 3.6% > stricture 1.3%); distal success 83–95%; proximal complications 23–68%; STAG <25%; Byars 30–70%; recurrent curvature 0–10%; recurrent UTI about 2%.
- About half of complications appear in year 1; follow up through puberty.
- Buccal graft 10% oversize; wait ≥6 months; staged success 30–75%; bladder mucosa prolapse up to 38%; tissue expander 95% / 41%; Cecil 6–8 weeks vs modified 9–12 months.

**Source errors and inconsistencies**
6. **POST points**: the body (A = distal plate, B = elbow, C = coronal margin) and the Fig. 56.8 legend (A = mucocutaneous junction, B = glanular knob, C = glanular–coronal junction) describe the same landmarks in different words. There is no conflict.
7. **Glans width thresholds**: <14 mm (risk factor, Bush) vs <15 mm (the authors' trigger for testosterone). These are different purposes, not a conflict; both are carried.
8. **AAP age recommendation inconsistent**: p. 1146 gives "6 and 12 months" (AAP 1996), but p. 1153 gives "the AAP-recommended age range of 6 to 18 months". Both are carried in their contexts (the 1996 elective repair window vs a range covering staged repairs).
9. **Curvature grading inconsistent**: the body grades mild <30°, moderate 30–45°, severe ≥45°, while the GMS score uses <30 / 30–60 / >60°. These are different systems, and both are tabulated.
10. **Spelling**: "Heineke-Mikulitz" (Fig. 56.11) vs "Heineke-Mikulicz" (text). The correct spelling is **Mikulicz**. Also "Gonzalbez" (Fig. 56.10) vs "Gosalbez" (Fig. 56.31). "systemic analyses" → systematic. "Surgical phot" (Fig. 56.15) → photo. "running suture line … either side of the late" (Fig. 56.22) → plate.

As a result, Proximal Hypospadias, Postoperative Management and Complications fall under TUBULARIZATION TECHNIQUES. The printed hierarchy is followed, as instructed, so the running header reads "TUBULARIZATION TECHNIQUES" over those pages.
12. **Earlier partial-record flags remain valid**: Fig. 56.39 vs text (Boston 53% vs 49%; Toronto 61%/38% vs 53%/32%); CHOP 40/81 = 49%, not 43% (corrected silently); Table 56.2 cross-reference to Fig. 56.40 should be Fig. 56.41; Key Points "60% in year 1" vs text "about half"; "qualify of life" → quality.

## Source errors and inconsistencies (corrected value carried in the documents)
1. p. 2 EAU risk groups: printed "doubling time ≥1 year AND grade group >4" (low) and "doubling time >1 year or GG 4–5" (high). Correct: **low = PSA-DT >1 year AND GG <4; high = PSA-DT ≤1 year OR GG 4–5** (consistent with p. 12).
2. p. 12: "grade group 4 after radiation" has lost its "<". Correct: **GG <4**.
3. NCCN threshold: body "≥0.1", Key Points ">0.1". Carried as **≥0.1**.
4. "American Urologic Associate" → **American Urological Association**; "European Association of Urologists" → **European Association of Urology**.
5. ASTRO expanded as "American Society for Therapeutic Radiology and Oncology" (the former name). Current name: **American Society for Radiation Oncology**.
6. p. 8 summary: "Phoenix (nadir $ 2)". Correct: **nadir + 2** (Key Points print "&" = +).
7. p. 8: "a PSA threshold of <0.2 may more appropriately identify recurrence" after LDR-BT contradicts the Key Points (≥0.2). Correct: **≥0.2 = recurrence; <0.2 at 4 years = cure**.
8. p. 8 (Zumsteg): "BCR defined as PSA nadir greater than 2 ng/mL". Correct: **nadir + 2 (Phoenix)**.
9. p. 8 (Romesser): bounce defined as "≥2 ng/mL above nadir". This is probably a dropped decimal (≥0.2). It is study data and was not carried.
10. p. 9 salvage RP: "grade group 3 or lower", then "(grade group >4)". Correct: **GG ≤3 (<4)**.
11. Units: "less than 50 ng/mL" (degarelix, p. 17) and "castrate levels (>50 ng/mL)" (p. 23). Correct: **<50 ng/dL**. "Undetectable PSA (<0.2 ng/dL)" (p. 23). Correct: **ng/mL**.
12. p. 24: "Observation … severely reduced life expectancy (>5 years)". Correct: **<5 years**.
13. p. 24 LATITUDE: "HR 0.67, 95% CI 0.51–0.76" is impossible (the HR lies outside its own CI). Correct: **HR 0.62** (as on p. 17 and Table 165.4). "LATTITUDE" is misspelt.
14. p. 24 "SQOG 9346" → **SWOG 9346**.
15. p. 21 venlafaxine is called an "SSRI". It is a **serotonin–noradrenaline reuptake inhibitor**. The Irani trial sentence ("benefit for all but superiority to medroxyprogesterone and cyproterone") is ambiguous. The trial found the **hormonal agents more effective than venlafaxine**, and that reading is carried.
16. p. 21 osteoporosis "2.5 SD below an age-specific reference mean" describes a Z-score. The T-score definition is against the **young-adult mean**.

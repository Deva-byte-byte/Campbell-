# Chapter 165 — Management of Recurrent and Newly Metastatic Prostate Cancer

<div class="top" markdown="1">
**IF YOU READ NOTHING ELSE**

- **BCR (biochemical recurrence) after RP (radical prostatectomy):** AUA (American Urological Association) **PSA ≥0.2**; NCCN (National Comprehensive Cancer Network) **≥0.1**/non-undetectable/two rises.
- **BCR after EBRT (external beam radiotherapy): Phoenix = nadir + ≥2 ng/mL**; after LDR (low-dose-rate) brachytherapy → **≥0.2**.
- **Salvage RT (radiotherapy) = only curative option** for post-RP BCR.
- **Early salvage at PSA 0.1–0.2 ≡ adjuvant** (RADICALS/RAVES/GETUG-AFU 17); ≥60% spared RT.
- **Salvage RT dose ≥64–65 Gy** fossa; **SAKK 09/10: 64 = 70 Gy**.
- **RTOG 0534:** bed → bed + ADT (androgen deprivation therapy) → pelvis + ADT = **70.9 → 81.3 → 87.4%** 5-year FFP (freedom from progression).
- **Imaging before salvage:** **PET (positron emission tomography)/CT excludes metastases; mpMRI (multiparametric MRI) finds local**.
- **Castrate testosterone <50 ng/dL**; rising PSA with castrate T = **CRPC (castration-resistant prostate cancer)**.
- **CHAARTED high volume:** **≥4 bone mets, ≥1 outside axial skeleton, or any visceral**.
- **mHSPC (metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer): ADT alone insufficient** → add docetaxel, abiraterone or ARTA (AR-targeting agent).

## CURATIVE INTENT APPROACHES TO BCR — AFTER PROSTATECTOMY

- **Cut-offs:** BCR by **10 years ~25–40%**; high-risk **~60% at 5 years**.
- **Threshold most predictive of metastasis:** **PSA ≥0.4 then rising**.
- **EAU (European Association of Urology) low-risk BCR:** PSA-DT (doubling time) **>1 year AND grade group <4**; **high-risk:** PSA-DT **≤1 year OR GG 4–5**.
- **Ultrasensitive PSA:** prognostic mainly in **intermediate/high risk**; ~half of early detectable values fall → **watch, don't treat**.
- **Only modifiable BCR risk factor:** **positive margin**; **>1 mm** length, **Gleason ≥4 at margin** worse.
- **pT3a margin-negative** lower BCR risk than **pT2 margin-positive**.
- **Nomograms:** **CAPRA pre-op; CAPRA-S post-op**; MSK tools → 15-year CSS (cancer-specific survival).
- **Pound (1999):** BCR → mets **8 years**; mets → death **5 years**.
- **Pound predictors:** time to BCR **<2 years**, PSA-DT **<10 months**, **Gleason 8–10**.
- **Post-RP PSA follow-up lifelong** — ~¼ recur **after 5 years**.
- **Bone scan/CT:** low yield **<PSA 10**.
- **Choline PET:** ~half positive **<PSA 1**; fluciclovine **poor <0.5**, ~nil **<0.2**.
- **PSMA > fluciclovine** at PSA <0.5.
- **EMPIRE-1:** fluciclovine-guided salvage → 3-year control **75.5 vs 63.0%**.
- **Salvage RT CSS benefit:** within **2 years of BCR**, PSA-DT **<6 months**.
- **Each 0.1 ng/mL** pre-salvage → **~2.6%** RFS lost.
- **Dose:** **>66 Gy** better in series; **~2%/Gy**; nodes **40–45 Gy**.
- **Margin >3 mm** in GG 3–5 → poorer salvage survival.
- **No MRI lesion before salvage** → higher failure (occult mets).
- **RTOG 9601:** RT **+ 2 years bicalutamide** → **OS 76.3 vs 71.3%** (12 years).
- **GETUG-AFU 16:** RT + **6 months goserelin** → BCR/MFS better, **OS not**.
- **Second BCR after salvage RT:** **~13–38%** → lifelong PSA.

## AFTER DEFINITIVE RADIATION THERAPY

- **Phoenix (ASTRO/RTOG 2005):** nadir + ≥2; valid **with ADT**.
- **LDR-BT cure:** **PSA <0.2 at 4 years**; BT dose **≥2× EBRT**.
- **PSA bounce:** rise **0.1–0.5**, **~9 months**, then falls → **favourable**; monitor.
- **Progression predictors:** short PSA-DT, stage, Gleason, **short interval to BCR**.
- **Post-RT CT:** useful only **PSA >20**; **TRUS (transrectal ultrasound) ≈ DRE**.
- **T2 MRI poor after RT** (diffuse low signal) → **DCE + DWI (mpMRI)**, AUC ~0.84.
- **Biopsy mandatory** before local salvage; post-RT **Gleason unreliable**.

## TREATMENT OF POSTRADIATION BCR

- **All salvage requires:** biopsy-proven local, imaging **M0**, **life expectancy 5–10 years+**.
- **EAU salvage RP criteria:** LE **≥10 years**, PSA **<10**, **GG ≤3**, **N0**, initial **cT1–2**.
- **Salvage RP:** robotic → better continence, fewer contractures/rectal injuries.
- **Salvage RP poor prognosis:** **GG ≥3**, pre-salvage **PSA ≥4**.
- **Salvage BT:** only **after EBRT**; **LDR ≈ HDR** (high-dose-rate); grade ≥3 GU **~17–24%**.
- **Cryotherapy selection:** PSA **<10 (ideally <4)**, PSA-DT **≥16 months**.
- **Cryotherapy:** **urethral warming** → incontinence single digits; fistula **1–3.4%**.
- **Cryo nadir <0.2** → avoids hormones.
- **HIFU (high-intensity focused ultrasound):** **≥2 years after EBRT**; fistula, **osteitis pubis**.
- **All modalities ~50–70%** biochemical control (Table 165.2).
- **After salvage for RT failure:** recurrence **>50%**.
- **Focal therapy failure → manage as radiation failure.**

## NONCURATIVE INTENT APPROACHES TO BCR

- **AUA:** **observation over ADT**; ADT if **PSA-DT <12 months** or high grade; **intermittent preferred**.
- **Untreated post-RP BCR (Pound):** only **~⅓ metastasise**.
- **EAU observation (post-RT):** interval to failure **>18 months**, **GG <4**.
- **Observation imaging:** **PET/CT preferred**, esp. PSA-DT **<12 months**.
- **AR-V7** (lacks LBD) → castration resistance, poor survival.

## METHODS OF ANDROGEN DEPRIVATION THERAPY

- **Orchidectomy:** testis = **95%** T; castrate **~6 hours**; **<20 ng/dL** by 12 hours.
- **Orchidectomy:** **fewer cardiac, PAD, fracture** events than LHRH agonists → offer if **lifelong ADT**.
- **NSAA (nonsteroidal antiandrogen) alone:** T **↑~1.5×** → **gynaecomastia** (aromatisation).
- **All NSAAs:** **hepatotoxicity, pneumonitis** (nilutamide **1–2%**).
- **Flutamide:** **diarrhoea**, 250 mg **tds**; inferior OS to DES.
- **Nilutamide:** **delayed dark adaptation**.
- **Bicalutamide:** **50 mg + LHRH only** approved; **150 mg** → worse OS localised.
- **Enzalutamide:** AFFIRM **2012**; **seizures <1%**; approved **all CRPC, mHSPC, high-risk nmHSPC**.
- **Apalutamide:** SPARTAN **2018**, MFS **40.5 vs 16.2** (HR 0.28); **rash, hypothyroidism, falls**.
- **Darolutamide:** ARAMIS **2019**, OS HR **0.68**; mHSPC **only with docetaxel**.
- **nmCRPC ARTA indication:** PSA-DT **≤10 months**.
- **AAWS (antiandrogen withdrawal syndrome):** **15–30%** PSA fall >50%, **3.5–5 months**; enzalutamide **0–10%**; **no OS gain**.
- **LHRH agonist flare:** LH **↑10×**, **10–20 days**; antiandrogen cover **21–28 days**.
- **Flare danger:** **spinal/weight-bearing** bone mets.
- **Agonist ↑FSH; antagonist ↓FSH.**
- **Antagonist:** LH **↓84% in 24 hours**, **no flare** → **cord compression**.
- **Abarelix:** allergy → observe **≥30 minutes**.
- **Degarelix:** castrate **>95% day 3** vs 0% leuprolide.
- **HERO (relugolix, oral):** castrate day 4 **56 vs 0%**; T recovery **54 vs 2%**.
- **~50%** fail to recover T **5 years** after LHRH agents.
- **Aminoglutethimide:** replace **cortisone + fludrocortisone**.
- **Ketoconazole:** castrate **<4 hours**, 200–400 mg **8-hourly** + **hydrocortisone**; emergency use.
- **Abiraterone:** **CYP17** (17α-hydroxylase, 17,20-lyase) + **prednisone 5 mg** → prevents ↑ACTH → **↓K⁺, ↑BP, oedema**.
- **COU-AA-301** OS **14.8 vs 10.9**; **COU-AA-302** rPFS **16.5 vs 8.3**.

## TIMING OF THERAPY

- **Primary ADT for localised disease: not recommended.**
- **Messing:** pN+ post-RP → immediate ADT **OS benefit** (bulky, dated).
- **EORTC 30891:** **30%** delayed never needed ADT; benefit if PSA **>50** or PSA-DT **<12 months**.
- **TOAD:** immediate ADT **OS HR 0.55**.
- **EMBARK entry:** PSA-DT **≤9 months**; PSA **≥1** post-RP or **≥2 above nadir** post-RT.
- **EMBARK MFS:** combo **87.3%** > enza **80.0%** > leuprolide **71.4%** (HR 0.42).
- **EMBARK pause/restart:** stop if PSA **<0.2** at week 37; restart **2** (post-RP) / **5** (post-RT).
- **Intermittent after RT (NCIC PR.7):** **non-inferior**; stop **<4**, restart **10**.
- **Intermittent in M1 (SWOG 9346):** **inconclusive → may be worse**; restart **20**.
- **Calcium 1000–1200 mg + vitamin D₃ 400–1000 IU** for all on ADT.
- **Treat bone if:** T-score **–1 to –2.5** + 10-year hip **>3%** or major **>20%**.
- **Denosumab 60 mg SC 6-monthly; zoledronate 4–5 mg IV yearly; alendronate 70 mg weekly.**
- **Hot flushes 50–80%**; **oestrogens most effective (~90%)**; venlafaxine **taper**.
- **Libido worse than erection**; **PDE5 inhibitors ineffective**.
- **ADT metabolic syndrome:** **subcutaneous fat, ↑HDL**; diabetes HR **1.44** (agonist).
- **Gynaecomastia:** DES/bicalutamide 150 **~50%**; orchidectomy/agonist **≤5%**.
- **Breast RT prophylaxis 10–15 Gy single** — useless once established.
- **Anaemia:** normochromic normocytic; Hb **<12** pre-ADT → worse survival; recovery **up to 1 year**.

## FOLLOW-UP ON ADT (NONMETASTATIC)

- **Review 3–6-monthly:** FBC, chemistry, PSA.
- **Faster time to PSA <0.2** → better survival.
- **HSD3B1 1245A>C (15–35%)** → ADT resistance; homozygous **~2× mets**.

## HORMONE SENSITIVE METASTATIC PROSTATE CANCER

- **Genetic testing all M1:** **>10%** DNA-repair mutations.
- **Median OS:** historical **~30 months** → now **>48 months**.
- **SWOG 9346 nadir at 7 months:** **≤0.2 → 75**; 0.2–4 → 44; **>4 → 13 months**.
- **Observation M1:** only if asymptomatic, LE **<5 years**.
- **CAB with NSAA:** **AUA against**.
- **Docetaxel 75 mg/m² 3-weekly × 6.**
- **CHAARTED:** OS **HR 0.61**, **high volume only**; **GETUG-AFU 15 negative**.
- **Docetaxel meta-analysis:** HR **0.77**, **94 fewer deaths/1000**.
- **LATITUDE high risk (≥2):** Gleason **≥8**, **≥3 bone lesions**, **visceral**; OS **HR 0.62**.
- **STAMPEDE G:** abiraterone OS **HR 0.63**.
- **ARCHES** rPFS **0.39**; **ENZAMET** OS **0.67** (vs NSAA); **TITAN** OS **0.67**.
- **ARTAs/abiraterone benefit regardless of volume.**
- **PEACE-1:** abiraterone + docetaxel → OS **HR 0.75**.
- **ARASENS:** darolutamide + docetaxel → OS **HR 0.68**.
- **STAMPEDE H:** prostate RT → OS **HR 0.68 low volume only**.
- **Oligometastatic = ≤3 sites** on PET.
- **STOMP:** ADT-free **21 vs 13 months**; **ORIOLE:** 6-month progression **19 vs 61%**.
- **mHSPC follow-up 3–6-monthly**; T with every PSA rise.

<div class="traps" markdown="1">
**CLASSIC TRAPS**

- **Trap:** detectable PSA after RP = metastatic course — **Actually:** ~half never fail clinically.
- **Trap:** Phoenix applies to brachytherapy — **Actually:** after LDR-BT use **≥0.2**.
- **Trap:** post-RT PSA bounce = failure — **Actually:** bounce predicts **better** outcome.
- **Trap:** NSAA alone produces castration — **Actually:** testosterone **rises ~1.5×**.
- **Trap:** LHRH antagonist needs antiandrogen cover — **Actually:** **no flare**; agonists need **21–28 days**.
- **Trap:** abiraterone prednisone replaces aldosterone — **Actually:** it **suppresses ACTH**.
- **Trap:** docetaxel helps all mHSPC — **Actually:** CHAARTED benefit **high volume only**.
- **Trap:** prostate RT for all M1 — **Actually:** **low-volume only** (STAMPEDE H).
- **Trap:** rising PSA on ADT = CRPC — **Actually:** **check testosterone <50 ng/dL** first.
- **Trap:** SPPORT (RTOG 0534) showed OS benefit — **Actually:** endpoint **freedom from progression**.

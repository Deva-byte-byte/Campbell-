## Instalment 1 — Definition and Boundaries, Primary vs Secondary Retroperitoneal Organs, Body Surface Landmarks, Flank Muscles, Psoas/Iliacus/Quadratus Lumborum/Erector Spinae, Table 78.1, Box 78.1, Figs 78.1–78.6 (pp. 1634–1639)

**The numbers to lock in**
- **Renal landmarks:** left upper pole at the 11th rib; right upper pole at the 12th rib; hila at L1; lower poles at L3–L4.
- **External oblique:** arises from ribs 5–12; its aponeurosis forms the inguinal ligament.
- **Psoas major:** T12–L5 → lesser trochanter; anterior rami L1–L3. Psoas minor (T12–L1) may be absent.
- **Eponyms:**
  - Gerota: anterior renal fascia (1895). Zuckerkandl: posterior renal fascia (1883). Toldt: conjoined visceral fasciae (1879/1893).
  - Lobstein (1829): first used "retroperitoneal tumour".
  - Bogros: space of Bogros.
  - Wickham (1979): pneumoretroperitoneum for a ureteric stone.
  - Gaur (1992): retroperitoneal videoscopic renal surgery.
- **Traps:**
  - The first part of the duodenum and the tail of the pancreas are NOT retroperitoneal.
  - Retroperitoneum ≠ extraperitoneal space.
  - The right kidney lies lower than the left.

## Instalment 2 — Spine, Lower Ribs, Intercostal Bundle, Supracostal Access, Lumbodorsal Fascia, Dorsal Lumbotomy, Fascial Strata and Retroperitoneal Spaces, Gastrointestinal Viscera and Mesentery, Table 78.2 (pp. 1640–1645)

**The numbers to lock in**
- **Vertebrae:** 7 cervical, 12 thoracic, 5 lumbar. Sacral cord segments begin at vertebral T12–L1.
- **Lower ribs:** 11th rib has no neck; 12th rib has no angle. The costovertebral ligament runs from the 12th rib to L1–L2.
- **Intercostal bundle:** vein → artery → nerve, from top down; the nerve is the least protected.
- **Supracostal vs subcostal access:** supracostal carries more complications; supra-12th access may cross the pleura.
- **Lumbodorsal fascia:** three layers that fuse at the 12th rib tip. Dorsal lumbotomy reaches the kidney without cutting muscle.
- **Strata → spaces:** outer stratum → transversalis fascia (posterior pararenal space); intermediate → renal fascia (perirenal space); inner → gut fusion fascia (anterior pararenal space).
- **Perirenal space:** cone-shaped and open inferiorly; may communicate across the midline below the hilum.
- **Duodenum:** 20–25 cm long; only D2 and D3 are retroperitoneal. D3 lies between the SMA and the aorta. Kocher manoeuvre for D2.
- **Traps:**
  - Perinephric collections track into the pelvis.
  - The posterior lamina is the thicker one.
  - Divide the hepatocolic and splenocolic ligaments sharply rather than retracting.
  - Close small mesenteric defects.

## Instalment 3 — CT Cross-Sectional Anatomy, Arterial System, Venous System, Left–Right Venous Asymmetry, Portal and Hepatic Drainage, Lymphatic System, Testicular Drainage, Table 78.3 (pp. 1646–1651)

**The numbers to lock in**
- **Aortic and IVC levels:** aorta enters at T12; celiac T12; SMA and renal arteries L1–L2; IMA L3–L4; bifurcation L4; IVC passes through the diaphragm at T8.
- **Arterial variants:** supernumerary renal arteries in at least 1 in 4 people; usually four pairs of lumbar arteries.
- **Venous variants:** right gonadal vein → right renal vein in up to 10%; right renal vein duplicated in one-sixth; IMV → SMV in 40%.
- **Safe to ligate:** IMA (marginal artery of Drummond), gonadal arteries, median sacral artery. **Not safe:** SMA.
- **Lymphatic drainage:**
  - Lymph flows cephalad and right → left.
  - Left testis → left para-aortic nodes. Right testis → interaortocaval nodes.
  - Cisterna chyli lies at L1–L2.
- **Traps:**
  - New right varicocele → image the retroperitoneum.
  - In reoperative surgery the IMV can be mistaken for the gonadal vein.
  - On the right, dissect lateral to the gonadal vein.
  - Right adrenal vein → posterolateral IVC.

## Instalment 4 — Autonomic Nervous System, Parasympathetic vs Sympathetic, Prevertebral and Hypogastric Plexuses, Somatic Lumbosacral Plexus, Nerve Injury in Urological Surgery, Table 78.4, Figs 78.37–78.41 (pp. 1652–1657)

**The numbers to lock in**
- **Autonomic outflow:** parasympathetic from cranial nerves III, VII, IX, X and S2–S4; sympathetic from cord segments T1–L2.
- **Lumbosacral plexus roots:** subcostal T12; iliohypogastric and ilioinguinal L1; genitofemoral L1–L2; lateral cutaneous nerve of thigh L2–L3; obturator and femoral L2–L4; sciatic L4–S3.
- **Nerve injuries:**
  - Obturator nerve: lateral-wall TURBT → adduction → perforation.
  - Genitofemoral nerve: psoas hitch or laparoscopic varicocelectomy.
  - Femoral nerve: retractor blades or low lithotomy.
  - Sciatic nerve: high lithotomy.
  - Hypogastric plexus: retrograde ejaculation.
- **Traps:**
  - The pelvic splanchnics are the only parasympathetic splanchnics.
  - The genitofemoral nerve, not the ilioinguinal, is motor to cremaster.
  - The lateral cutaneous nerve of the thigh is purely sensory.

### Source errors and inconsistencies (corrected versions carried silently in the PDFs)
1. **Quadratus lumborum position:** the text says "posterior and medial to the psoas". It lies **posterolateral** to psoas.
2. **Quadratus lumborum origin:** the text says "L5 and the iliac fossa", but Table 78.2 says "iliac crest". The **iliac crest** is correct, and the table value is carried.
3. **Iliacus origin:** the text says "the caudal aspect of the iliac fossa". Iliacus arises from the upper (cranial) iliac fossa. The notes say "iliac fossa and lateral sacrum" without the incorrect qualifier.
4. **Sympathetic outflow:** the text says preganglionic fibres "originate between the first thoracic and the second lumbar *vertebral* levels". It should be **spinal cord segments** T1–L2. This matters because the chapter itself warns to distinguish vertebral from segmental level.
5. **Key Points, vasculature:** "The blood supply to the adrenal *artery*" should read **adrenal gland**.
6. **Infrarenal IVC:** "The infrarenal portion runs parallel and *inferior* to the aorta" is ambiguous. The notes carry "alongside the aorta, to its right".
7. **Testicular arteries:** the text says they run "anterior to the psoas, IVC …". Only the **right** testicular artery crosses the IVC. The notes carry this qualification.
8. **Great vessel compartment:** "between the perirenal space" should read "between the perirenal **spaces**".
9. **Box 78.1 attribution:**
   - "Miralis" should be **Mirilas**.
   - The citation "Am Surg 78(11) … 2009" gives the wrong volume: the 2009 volume is 75.
   - The attribution is carried as "Mirilas and Skandalakis, 2009".
10. **Table 78.1 spellings:** "Poirer" should be **Poirier**, and "Altmeir" should be **Altemeier**.

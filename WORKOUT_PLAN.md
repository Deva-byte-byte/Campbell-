# 8-Week Home Workout Plan for Fat Loss (no equipment)

## Starting point

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Height | 171 cm |
| Weight | 65–66 kg |
| BMI | ~22.4 (normal range) |
| Daily steps | ~1,000 (very low) |
| Equipment | None |

**What this means:** your weight is already in the healthy range, so the goal is to lose fat
(a realistic target is 61–63 kg) while keeping muscle. Your biggest problem isn't a lack of
workouts. It's that you barely move during the day, at about 1k steps. Walking more will
burn more calories than any single workout, so **steps are the #1 priority** and
strength training is #2.

Realistic rate: **0.25–0.5 kg per week**, or about 2–4 kg over 8 weeks.

---

## 1. Daily step targets (most important)

| Week | Daily step target | How |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | 3,000–4,000 | 10-min walk after each meal |
| 3–4 | 5,000–6,000 | Add one 20-min walk |
| 5–6 | 7,000–8,000 | Walk during phone calls, take stairs |
| 7–8 | 8,000–10,000 | One 30–40 min brisk walk daily |

If you can't go outside, march or walk in place indoors. 10 minutes is roughly 1,000–1,200 steps.

## 2. Weekly schedule

| Day | Session | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | **Strength A** | 30–35 min |
| Tue | **Cardio / HIIT** | 20–25 min |
| Wed | **Strength B** | 30–35 min |
| Thu | Active recovery: mobility + long walk | 20 min + walk |
| Fri | **Strength A** | 30–35 min |
| Sat | **Cardio / HIIT** | 20–25 min |
| Sun | Rest (still hit your step target) | — |

(Week 2 swaps: Mon B, Wed A, Fri B, so you alternate.)

### Warm-up (5 min, before every session)
Arm circles 30s, hip circles 30s, march in place 1 min, bodyweight squats ×10,
inchworms ×5, jumping jacks (or step-jacks) 1 min.

### Strength A: legs + push
| Exercise | Weeks 1–2 | Weeks 3–4 | Weeks 5–8 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bodyweight squat | 3×12 | 3×15 | 4×15 (3-sec lowering) |
| Push-up (knee / incline on bed if needed) | 3×6–8 | 3×10 | 4×10–12 (full push-ups) |
| Reverse lunge (each leg) | 2×8 | 3×10 | 3×12 |
| Pike push-up or chair dips | 2×6 | 3×8 | 3×10 |
| Plank | 3×20s | 3×30s | 3×45s |

### Strength B: hips + back + core
| Exercise | Weeks 1–2 | Weeks 3–4 | Weeks 5–8 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Glute bridge | 3×15 | 3×20 | 3×12 single-leg (each) |
| Superman / back extension | 3×10 | 3×12 | 3×15 (2s hold) |
| Split squat (each leg) | 2×8 | 3×10 | 3×12 (Bulgarian, foot on bed/chair) |
| Towel row under a sturdy table, or doorframe row | 3×8 | 3×10 | 4×10 |
| Dead bug (each side) | 3×8 | 3×10 | 3×12 |
| Side plank (each side) | 2×15s | 3×20s | 3×30s |

Rest 45–75s between sets. When the top of a rep range feels easy, move to the harder column.

### Cardio / HIIT (Tue & Sat)
Circuit with **30s work / 30s rest**, 4 rounds (weeks 1–4) → 40s/20s, 5 rounds (weeks 5–8):
1. Jumping jacks (low-impact: step-jacks)
2. High knees (or fast march)
3. Mountain climbers
4. Squat to calf raise
5. Shadow boxing

Rest 1 min between rounds. If HIIT feels too hard early on, do a 30-min brisk walk instead.

### Active recovery (Thu)
Cat-cow, hip flexor stretch, hamstring stretch, child's pose, thoracic rotations:
about 1 min each, ×2. Then a long walk.

## 3. Nutrition (this drives fat loss)

- **Small deficit:** eat ~300–500 kcal below maintenance. Don't crash diet.
- **Protein:** ~100–110 g/day (about 1.6 g per kg). Sources: eggs, paneer, dal, chicken, fish, curd/Greek yogurt, soya chunks, tofu.
- Fill half your plate with vegetables. Cut back on sugary drinks, fried snacks and late-night eating.
- Water: 2.5–3 L/day. Sleep: 7–8 hours.

### Rules for adjusting
- Weekly average not dropping for 2 weeks → add 1,500 steps/day **or** cut ~150 kcal.
- Losing more than 0.75 kg/week → eat a little more (to protect muscle).
- Waist shrinking while weight stays the same → that's fat loss. Keep going.

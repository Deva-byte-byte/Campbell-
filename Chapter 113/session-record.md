## The numbers to lock in

**Instalment 1**
- BESIDE continence: combination 46.0% vs solifenacin 5 mg 37.9% vs solifenacin 10 mg 40.2%; combination > solifenacin 5 but not > solifenacin 10.
- SYNERGY: 12 weeks, six arms; SYNERGY II: 12 months. Solifenacin 5 + mirabegron 50 > each monotherapy.
- MILAI: mirabegron 25 → 50 mg added after ≥4 weeks of solifenacin 2.5/5 mg; 16 weeks.
- MILAI II: after 6 weeks of mirabegron 50, add solifenacin 5, propiverine 20, imidafenacin 0.2 or tolterodine ER 4 mg; dose doubled after 8 weeks (not tolterodine ER); 52 weeks.
- Vibegron/tolterodine 100/4 mg: phase IIb only.
- NP in about 30% of men aged ≥60 with BPH; desmopressin 0.1 mg for men aged ≥65; ≥50% fall in nocturnal voids.
- DIGNITY onabotA 200 U (NDO) vs EMBARK 100 U (idiopathic OAB).
- Traps: combination beats solifenacin 10; mirabegron combines with any antimuscarinic; oestrogen order doesn't matter; EMBARK permits a user comparison.

**Instalment 2**
- FDA ban on systemic α-agonists for SUI: December 2003 (haemorrhagic stroke).
- BoNT/B: 2,500–15,000 U; ≤3 months; ≤10 weeks at 5,000 U.
- Flupirtine 400 → 600 mg: halted for hepatotoxicity. ZD0947 25 mg: null. Nimodipine 30 mg: null.
- Elocalcitol 75/150 µg, phase IIb, 4 weeks. Clenbuterol 20 µg twice daily. PSD503: phenylephrine 20%.
- Prostatic onabotA RCT entry: IPSS >12 with Qmax 5–15, or IPSS >14 with Qmax 4–15; equal to saline.
- PRX302: 0.6 µg/g; entry IPSS ≥15, Qmax ≤12 mL/s, prostate 30–100 mL; IPSS ↓~9, Qmax ↑3 mL/s at 3 months.
- NX-1207: phase III negative.
- Traps: PSD503 failed on acceptability, not efficacy; calcium channel blockers do not impair voiding clinically.

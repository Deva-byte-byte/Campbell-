## The numbers to lock in

### Instalments 1–2
- **Pregnancy**: 8–12 mm Hg; second trimester; Palmer point; difficult after 20 weeks.
- **Transfusion**: nephrectomy 3–12%; prostatectomy 2.5%; partial nephrectomy 6–7%.
- **ERAS (2001)**: solids 6 h, carbohydrate drink 2 h; stay −30%, complications −50%.
- **Positioning**: lower leg 45°; axillary roll 3–4 fingerbreadths below the axilla; kidney rest ≤20–30 min.
- **Veress**: 14 G, 2-mm outer diameter, 70 or 120 mm; ≤2 mm Hg at 2 L/min; insufflate at 9 L/min to 15 mm Hg; free flow <8–10 mm Hg; advancement 1 cm; head down 10–20° for umbilical entry.
- **Absolute contraindications**: uncorrectable coagulopathy; untreated intestinal obstruction; abdominal wall infection; massive haemoperitoneum or haemoretroperitoneum; generalised peritonitis; suspected malignant ascites. **Relative**: obesity, previous surgery, fibrosis, organomegaly, ascites, pregnancy, hernia, aneurysm.
- **Eponyms**: Palmer point (left subcostal, midclavicular line); Osler.
- **Traps**: no shoulder braces in Trendelenburg; never insufflate through a hernia site; obese umbilicus → perpendicular needle.

### Instalments 3–4
- **Access**: Hasson 2 cm; hand port 6.5–7.5 cm; retroperitoneal 2–2.5 cm below the 12th rib tip; extraperitoneal 1.5–2 cm.
- **Balloons**: 800 mL / 40 pumps; Gaur glove on 8-Fr catheter at 110 mm Hg; 30-Fr Amplatz sheath.
- **Trocars**: 3–20 mm diameter, 5–20 cm long; blunt vs bladed bleeding 3% vs 9%, complications 3% vs 10%; primary trocar 70° caudad for the pelvis; 20 mm Hg for first-trocar insertion.
- **Geometry**: <55° to the horizontal; 25–45° between instruments; robotic ports 8–10 cm apart.
- **Energy**: thermal spread monopolar 5–7 mm, bipolar 1–6, LigaSure 1–3, ultrasonic ≈1; LigaSure ≤7 mm vessels; ultrasonic 25–55 kHz, tissue <80 °C, blade >200 °C, cooling up to 45 s.
- **Staples**: 2.0 grey / 2.5 white vascular; 3.8 blue / 4.8 green thick tissue.
- **Lapra-Ty**: 3.5 mm, 14 days, avoid with 4-0.
- **Gold standards and commonest**: Hasson for adhesions; blunt trocars; ProGrasp most used.
- **Eponyms**: Hasson; Gaur; Kittner; Satinsky; Carter-Thomason.
- **Traps**: metal trocar with plastic retainer; firing a stapler across clips; repeated LigaSure applications; ultrasonic jaws on bowel.

### Instalments 5–6
- **Hem-o-lok**: ≥2 clips, 2-mm cuff; 10 mm of tissue through 5-mm port, 16 mm through 10-mm.
- **Specimens**: LapSac 2 kg; extraction 5–7 cm.
- **Robot**: EndoWrist 7 degrees of freedom, 90° articulation, 10 uses; robot failure <1%, malfunction 0.4%.
- **Exit**: inspect at 5 mm Hg; fascial closure rules as tabulated; skin with 4-0 subcuticular.
- **Pressure**: commonest 15 mm Hg, optimal 12, oliguria reduced at 10; authors use 8 with AirSeal; 20 mm Hg raises filling volume.
- **Physiology**: Table 18.1 patterns; medullary flow rises to 20 mm Hg; mannitol 12.5–25 g; dopamine 2 µg/kg/min; urine output >100 mL/h; arterial gases in COPD if >1 h; CO₂ absorption plateaus at 30–60 min.
- **Complication rates**: 4% historical; 13–22% contemporary; mortality 0.03–0.08%.
- **Preperitoneal insufflation**: pressure spike at about 500 mL.
- **Gas embolism**: end-tidal CO₂ rises, then saturation falls, then end-tidal CO₂ falls; left lateral decubitus, head down, 100% oxygen.
- **Insufflants**: CO₂ standard; helium for COPD; N₂O never with diathermy.
- **Traps**: CVP unreliable; treat oliguria with drugs, not fluid; hypercarbia peaks in recovery.

### Instalments 7–8
- **Thoracic gas**: pneumopericardium 0.8%; pneumothorax 1.6–4% (16 G, second or third intercostal space, midclavicular line).
- **Vascular injury**: 0.11–2%; venous bleeding → raise to 25 mm Hg.
- **Bowel injury**: thermal necrosis up to 18 days; free air up to 9 days; monopolar resection margin 6 cm; bipolar injury >½ circumference → resection; irrigate with 4–5 L.
- **Port sites**: midline or ≥6 cm lateral (epigastric vessels).
- **Nerve injury**: 2.8% in survey.
- **Hernia**: hand-port 4–7%; transverse incision 5.4% → 0.4%; bladed → blunt 1.8% → 0.19%.
- **Postoperative**: compression stockings 48–72 h; heparin 5,000 units 2 h before, then every 12 h; hand-port wound infection 9%; rhabdomyolysis 0.4% (retroperitoneal 1%), CK >5,000, kidney rest <1 h, cases <5 h; complications after 3 weeks rare.
- **Training**: E-BLUS 2013; mini-residency 5 days; 3-0 SH needle for practice.
- **When to convert**: major arterial or aortic injury; uncontrolled venous injury. **When not to operate**: minor late thermal injury improving with antibiotics and elemental diet.
- **Traps**: leave the trocar in an injured vessel; always repair a bladder injury; pinch the caval walls, do not press on top; the scope leaves the abdomen last.

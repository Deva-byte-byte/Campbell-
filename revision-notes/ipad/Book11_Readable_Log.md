## Ch 95 · Urinary Lithiasis: Etiology, Epidemiology and Pathogenesis
# ch95 rewrite log
**Missed-in-paper points added**
- Stone formation (P2-057): free- vs fixed-particle theories; vasa recta hypothesis; infection/SWL converting calcium oxalate to brushite formers; matrix substance A only in formers, matrix role undetermined.
- RTA (P2-060): distal RTA genetics (SLC4A1 dominant, H⁺-ATPase recessive with deafness); type 2 spares citrate; hypercalciuria from acidotic bone demineralisation; stones lead to diagnosis in up to half.
- Uric acid (P2-058): ~one-third of stones in type 2 diabetes; >1200 mg/L soluble at pH 6.5.
- Cystine/struvite (P1-136/P4-062): no nutritional deficiency (oligopeptide absorption); less than half of staghorns are infection stones; voided culture unreliable.

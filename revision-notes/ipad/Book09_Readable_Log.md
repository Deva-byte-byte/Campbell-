## Ch 70 · Surgical Management of Male Infertility
# Ch 70 rewrite log

## Missed-in-paper points added
- P1-106 (testis biopsy/TESA): biopsy usually bilateral, planned as TESE with on-site andrology lab; avoid blind 14-G core in scarred scrotum.
- P3-062 (EDO/TURED): minimal coagulation and 7 days antibiotics; avoid high-pressure vesiculography (secondary epididymal obstruction); reflux documented by VCUG/semen creatinine; epididymitis suppression, vasectomy/epididymectomy if chronic; pseudoephedrine 60 mg 90 min before or alkalinised urinary sperm.
- P2-044 (sperm retrieval): PESA pregnancy ~half of open and often too few to freeze; open TESE most consistent; TESA 10–20 passes; fresh testicular sperm may beat frozen in SCI.

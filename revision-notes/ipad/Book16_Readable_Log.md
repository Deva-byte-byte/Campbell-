## Ch 150 · Evaluation & Nonsurgical Management of BPH
# ch150 rewrite log
**Missed-in-paper points added**
- P3-108 (IPSS): with a severe baseline, percentage reduction is more reliable than the 3-point threshold.
- P2-138 (UDS): DO on cystometry identifies antimuscarinic responders; DUA predicts long-term surgical failure; contractility is independent of treatment and of obstruction duration.
- P1-169 (prostate size): volume required before 5-ARI and any surgery; median lobe guides MIST choice; ALTESS PV >49 mL predicts AUR but weakly BOO; IPP ≥10 mm predicts failed TWOC; BWT/DWT/UEBW/penile cuff/NIRS accurate but none replaces PFS.
- P2-128 (medical management): antimuscarinic PVR ceiling 200 mL, avoid oxybutynin in elderly; hexane-extract Serenoa (Permixon) is an EAU option.
- P2-136/P2-133 (tamsulosin, silodosin): no α1-blocker clearly more effective, doxazosin 39% progression cut but not AUR/surgery; ALLHAT (never sole antihypertensive, never two α-blockers); elderly falls/fractures, severe hypotension mostly in first 8 weeks.
- P2-134 (dutasteride): 5-ARIs spare the HPG axis (other androgen manipulations ineffective/toxic); finasteride DHT ~70%, PV 18–20%, PLESS AUR 57%/surgery 55%, no head-to-head difference.

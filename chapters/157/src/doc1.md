# Chapter 157 — Diagnosis and Staging of Prostate Cancer — Numbers, Gold Standards and Traps

<div class="top">
<p class="bt">IF YOU READ NOTHING ELSE</p>

- **Prostate-specific antigen (PSA) risk is continuous** — no level is risk-free; PSA 0–1 ng/mL → ~9% cancer (PCPT, Prostate Cancer Prevention Trial).
- **ERSPC (European Randomised Study of Screening for Prostate Cancer)**: **20% ↓ cancer mortality** at 16 yr; invite 570, diagnose 18 → 1 death prevented.
- **PLCO (Prostate, Lung, Colorectal and Ovarian trial)**: **no mortality benefit** — contamination, low power.
- **Most important PSA determinant: prostate disease** (cancer, benign prostatic hyperplasia [BPH], prostatitis).
- **5α-reductase inhibitor (5-ARI) ≥12 mo → PSA ×2**; ×2.3 at 2 yr, ×2.5 at 7 yr.
- **Repeat raised PSA before biopsy**; no antibiotics for PSA alone.
- **Free PSA percentage (%fPSA) 25%** at PSA 4–10 → ~95% cancers found, ~20% biopsies spared.
- **Seminal vesicle invasion = T3b**; non-regional nodes = **M1a**.
- **Grade group (GG) 2 = 3+4, GG3 = 4+3**.
- **Low/intermediate risk → no routine CT or bone scan**; high risk → bone scan + CT or multiparametric MRI (mpMRI).
</div>

## DIAGNOSIS

- **Screening decision: shared** — USPSTF (U.S. Preventive Services Task Force) grade C 55–69; **against ≥70**.
- **USPSTF history**: 2008 against ≥75 → 2012 **grade D** → 2018 grade C.
- **AUA (American Urological Association)**: 55–69, **2-yearly** interval.
- **NCCN (National Comprehensive Cancer Network)**: baseline PSA **at 45**, repeat 1–4 yr.
- **American Cancer Society**: from **50**; earlier if Black or first-degree relative <65.
- **Overtreatment worst >65 yr**; mean age at diagnosis ~66.
- **PSA era**: mortality **↓ >40%**.
- **Malmö**: PSA in 40s predicts metastasis/death **>25 yr later**.
- **Biopsy thresholds**: 4 (original FDA) · **2.5–3** (common) · **3** (ERSPC) · **10 if >70 yr**.
- **Digital rectal examination (DRE)**: little value at **PSA <3**; most urologists **combine PSA + DRE**.
- **Suspicious DRE ≈ doubles biopsy yield**: PSA 4–10 → 17–32% vs **45–51%**; >10 → 43–65% vs **70–90%**.
- **PSA ↑**: volume ≫ age; Black ethnicity; biopsy, massage, transurethral resection.
- **PSA ↓**: hypogonadism, obesity, statins, 5-ARI, BPH surgery.
- **DRE → no false-positive PSA** (within assay error).
- **Ejaculation**: repeat after **48 h abstinence** if minimally raised.
- **Finasteride 1 mg = 5 mg** for PSA lowering.
- **5-ARI**: baseline PSA first; alternatively on-treatment **nadir = new baseline**.
- **PSA density (PSAD) ≥0.15** → biopsy (PSA 4–10, normal DRE); also active surveillance eligibility.
- **PSA velocity (PSAV) >0.75 ng/mL/yr** (PSA 4–10); **0.4** at lower PSA.
- **PSAV >0.35** 10–15 yr pre-diagnosis → lethal cancer; **>2** in year before → cancer death after treatment.
- **%fPSA 25% ≈ PSAD 0.078** in specificity, no volume needed.
- **%fPSA unaffected** by ethnicity or 5-ARI.
- **Complexed PSA (cPSA)**: specificity > total PSA, ≈ %fPSA.
- **proPSA → cancer; benign PSA (bPSA) → BPH**; human kallikrein 2 (hK2) tracks grade.
- **Prostate Health Index (phi)** = tPSA + fPSA + [−2]proPSA; FDA-approved.
- **4Kscore** = tPSA, fPSA, intact PSA, hK2.
- **Prostate cancer gene 3 (PCA3), TMPRSS2:ERG**: urine; aggressiveness data conflicting.
- **ConfirmMDx**: methylation of **benign biopsy tissue** → avoids repeat biopsy.
- **tPSA alone rarely triggers biopsy** → add biomarkers + clinical risk.

## STAGING

- **Pathological > clinical stage** for accuracy and outcome prediction.
- **Post-prostatectomy predictors**: grade, margins, extracapsular extension, seminal vesicle invasion, nodes.
- **TNM**: adopted **1975**; current **2017**; Whitmore–Jewett historical.
- **T1a ≤5% · T1b >5%** resected tissue; **T1c** needle-biopsy detected.
- **T2a ≤½ one side · T2b >½ one side · T2c both sides**.
- **T4**: fixed or invades sphincter, rectum, bladder, levator, pelvic wall.
- **M1a nodes · M1b bone · M1c other ± bone**.
- **Organ-confined**: ~80% PSA <4 · ~66% 4–10 · <50% >10.
- **Node-positive**: ~20% PSA >20 · ~75% PSA >50.
- **High-grade tumours → less PSA per gram**.
- **Prostatic acid phosphatase**: obsolete.
- **Most important biopsy finding: grade**; Gleason most used.
- **Gleason** = predominant + second pattern, 2–10; tertiary 5 upgrades (2005).
- **Grade groups**: 1 ≤6 · 2 3+4 · 3 4+3 · 4 = 8 · 5 = 9–10.
- **Cribriform/intraductal, % positive cores** → prognosis, active surveillance selection.
- **D'Amico low**: T1–2a + PSA ≤10 + Gleason ≤6 → 83% disease-free at 10 yr.
- **D'Amico intermediate**: T2b or PSA >10–<20 or Gleason 7 → 46%.
- **D'Amico high**: T2c or PSA >20 or Gleason 8–10 → 29%.
- **MRI** before repeat biopsy after negative biopsy; key points: before any biopsy.
- **Suspicious MRI lesion → targeted biopsy** (MRI–transrectal ultrasound fusion).
- **mpMRI** = T2 + diffusion-weighted + dynamic contrast-enhanced.
- **PSMA (prostate-specific membrane antigen) PET**: ⁶⁸Ga-PSMA-11, ¹⁸F-DCFPyL — approved for **initial high-risk staging**.
- **Genomic tests**: Oncotype DX **17 genes** · Decipher **22 RNA** · Prolaris cell-cycle.
- **Pelvic lymph node dissection (PLND)**: no consistent oncological benefit → **nomogram-guided, staging only**.

<div class="traps">
<p class="bt">CLASSIC TRAPS</p>

- **Trap:** normal PSA excludes cancer — **Actually:** ~9% at PSA 0–1.
- **Trap:** PLCO proves screening futile — **Actually:** contaminated control arm.
- **Trap:** seminal vesicle invasion is T4 — **Actually:** T3b.
- **Trap:** non-regional nodes are N1 — **Actually:** M1a.
- **Trap:** bilateral impalpable cancer is T2c — **Actually:** T1c.
- **Trap:** 3+4 and 4+3 same group — **Actually:** GG2 vs GG3.
- **Trap:** 5-ARI lowers %fPSA — **Actually:** tPSA halves, %fPSA unchanged.
- **Trap:** fPSA higher in cancer — **Actually:** lower.
- **Trap:** D'Amico low risk needs one criterion — **Actually:** all three.
- **Trap:** stage low-risk with bone scan — **Actually:** not routinely.
</div>

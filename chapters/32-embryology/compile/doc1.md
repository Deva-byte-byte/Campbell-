<p class="boxt">IF YOU READ NOTHING ELSE</p>

- **Type 2 5α-reductase** → DHT (dihydrotestosterone); deficiency → **severe hypospadias, rudimentary prostate**
- **Whole urethral epithelium = endoderm (urogenital sinus, UGS)**, glans included

- **Cryptorchidism ~3%** newborn males; hydrocele/hernia commoner
- **46,XY streak gonad → remove** (gonadoblastoma)
- **Entire vaginal epithelium = UGE** (urogenital sinus epithelium; Bulmer)
- **Weigert–Meyer:** upper pole **caudal-medial → ectopic**; lower pole **lateral → VUR** (vesicoureteral reflux)

## DEVELOPMENT OF THE EXTERNAL GENITALIA

- **Timeline:** indifferent to **8–9 wk** → coronal sulcus **11–12 wk** → folds fused **16 wk** → complete **18 wk** → chordee gone **20 wk**
- **First sign of masculinisation:** ↑ **anogenital distance**
- **Germ layers:** ectoderm → skin/prepuce; mesoderm → corpora; **endoderm → urethra**
- **Double zipper:** **canalisation** (proliferation, no apoptosis) → **fold fusion** (shaft)
- **Glans urethra:** **direct canalisation** of plate, not fold fusion
- **Glenister:** ectodermal-intrusion theory of glans urethra — **outdated**
- **Innervation:** somatic **S2–S4 pudendal**; sympathetic **L1–L2**; parasympathetic **S2–S4**
- **Dorsal nerves:** **11 and 1 o'clock**, fan ventrally to spongiosum; **12 o'clock free**
- **Alcock's canal:** pudendal canal carrying pudendal nerve
- **Hypospadias tetrad:** ectopic meatus, **dorsal hooded foreskin**, ventral skin deficiency, ± curvature
- **Hypospadias = developmental arrest**, not deformation
- **Aetiology:** mostly unknown; **genetic susceptibility + endocrine disruptors**
- **Operate if:** mild–moderate curvature → **dorsal 12 o'clock plication**
- **Chordee without hypospadias:** penoscrotal webbing
- **Duplicated urethra:** plate splitting; complete vs **Y-duplication**; **Sonic hedgehog**

## DEVELOPMENT OF THE FEMALE EXTERNAL GENITALIA

- **Clitoris = penis minus urethra**
- **Canalisation androgen-independent**; **fold fusion androgen-dependent**
- **Clitoral nerves:** densest dorsally; **12 o'clock nerve-free**
- **CAH (congenital adrenal hyperplasia):** autosomal recessive, ↓ cortisol → virilised 46,XX; urethra can be **male-identical**
- **CAH clue:** **impalpable gonads + hyperpigmentation**

## DEVELOPMENT OF THE HUMAN PROSTATE

- **Buds:** **10 wk** (9.5–10) from **FOXA1+ UGE**, gutters **lateral to verumontanum**
- **Branching:** from **~11 wk**; sets adult **zonal anatomy**
- **Verumontanum:** mesoderm (Wolffian + utricle) meets endoderm (UGE)
- **Six stages:** prebud → buds → branching → canalisation → luminal/basal → secretion
- **Canalisation:** proximal → distal
- **Markers:** basal **TP63, KRT5/14**; luminal **KRT8/18**
- **Smooth muscle:** **ventral first**, then lateral/dorsal
- **DHT:** **~10×** AR (androgen receptor) affinity vs testosterone; **5αR2 in mesenchyme**
- **No functional AR → no prostate**
- **Mesenchyme instructs epithelium:** UGM (urogenital sinus mesenchyme) induces prostate even in bladder epithelium
- **Squamous metaplasia:** needs **ESR1 in stroma + epithelium**; normal fetal (maternal oestrogen)
- **Jost / Price:** animal proof of androgen-dependent prostate
- **Seminal vesicle:** **Wolffian diverticulum, 10–12 wk**; side ducts months 4–5; nerves 13 wk

## DEVELOPMENT OF THE GONADS

- **Germinal ridge 4 wk**; **PGCs (primordial germ cells) yolk sac wk 3** → hindgut mesentery → ridge
- **Primary sex cords wk 6**; seminiferous cords from **mesonephric tubules**
- **Testis cords ~6 wk**; **ovarian cortex/medulla ~8 wk**
- **Gonads identical 7–8 wk**; testis becomes spherical, ovary oblong
- **Efferent ductules:** **5–12 mesonephric tubules**; full continuity **3rd month**
- **Leydig testosterone:** hCG (human chorionic gonadotrophin) early → fetal pituitary later
- **Wolffian → epididymis, vas, seminal vesicle** (wk 8–12)
- **Appendix testis = Müllerian**; **appendix epididymis = Wolffian**; paradidymis = mesonephric
- **Ovary:** germ-cell nests **10–11 wk**; primary follicles **~20 wk**; secondary at **term**; antral postnatal
- **Oocytes:** meiosis I in fetus → **arrest to puberty**
- **Homologues:** Cowper ↔ **Bartholin**; Littre ↔ **Skene**; spongiosum ↔ **vestibular bulb**
- **Gartner duct:** Wolffian remnant, anterolateral vaginal wall
- **Descent:** transabdominal **wk 10–15**; inguinoscrotal from **7th month**; migration **3–5 cm**
- **CSL (cranial suspensory ligament) regression androgen-dependent**; **gubernacular swelling androgen-independent**
- **Inguinoscrotal phase:** androgen via **genitofemoral nerve → CGRP** (calcitonin gene-related peptide)
- **INSL3 (insulin-like peptide 3):** Leydig; knockout → **intra-abdominal cryptorchidism** only
- **Ovary:** descends **3rd month**; gubernaculum → **ovarian + round ligaments**
- **Retained CSL → intra-abdominal testis**; ectopic gubernaculum → perineal/femoral/penile
- **Processus not closed → hernia/hydrocele**; **not involuted → acquired (ascending) testis**
- **Streak gonad:** **45,X / 46,XX / 46,XY**; no MIS, no testosterone → female; **HRT (hormone replacement)**
- **Ovotestis:** ducts follow ipsilateral gonad; female fertility possible, **no spermatogenesis**
- **PMDS (persistent Müllerian duct syndrome):** 46,XY, **MIS receptor defect**; found at exploration for **bilateral abdominal testes**
- **Müllerian MIS sensitivity:** **9–13 wk**

## MÜLLERIAN STRUCTURES (FEMALE INTERNAL GENITALIA)

- **Müllerian ducts start wk 5**; **Wolffian duct = obligatory guidewire**
- **Fusion → uterovaginal canal wk 8**; **septum gone wk 9**
- **Carnegie stage 22** ≈ 54 days, indifferent stage
- **Vaginal canalisation:** starts **~15 wk**, done **18–19 wk**
- **Markers:** **PAX2 = Müllerian**; **FOXA1 = UGE**; switch at **vaginal–cervical junction**
- **Koff:** mixed, Müllerian-predominant; **Bulmer:** all UGE — **supported**
- **Hymen:** UGE-derived
- **Transverse vaginal septum:** failed canalisation, **~1:80,000**, middle/upper third junction
- **Imperforate hymen = commonest vaginal obstruction**
- **Didelphys:** failed fusion; **unicornuate:** one duct absent; **septum persists** → obstruction/duplication
- **OHVIRA (obstructed hemi-vagina and ipsilateral renal anomalies):** post-menarche cyclic pain, discharge; acute abdomen/**tubo-ovarian abscess**
- **OHVIRA kidney:** usually **dysplastic**, not agenetic; **true agenesis → absent hemi-trigone**
- **Do not assume agenesis:** ectopic ureter → **vaginal leakage after septum resection**

## BLADDER: URETERAL DEVELOPMENT

- **Cloaca divided wk 5–6** by **urorectal septum** → anorectal canal + UGS
- **Anorectal malformations:** from **cloacal membrane**, not septum
- **Allantois → urachal remnant**
- **Wolffian entry** divides vesicourethral canal (bladder, pelvic urethra) from caudal UGS
- **Common excretory ducts join UGS wk 5–6**; apoptosis frees ureters
- **Trigone:** **mostly bladder smooth muscle**, minor ureteral input
- **Bud too low** → lateral orifice → **VUR**; **bud too high** → caudal-medial → **ectopic**
- **Male ectopic sites:** bladder neck, verumontanum, **vas/Wolffian derivatives**
- **Female ectopic sites:** **Gartner duct cyst**, vaginal vestibule

<div class="traps" markdown="1">
<p class="boxt">CLASSIC TRAPS</p>

- **Trap:** glanular urethra ectodermal — **Actually:** endodermal UGS throughout
- **Trap:** urethral groove opens by apoptosis — **Actually:** proliferation; no apoptosis
- **Trap:** canalisation needs androgen — **Actually:** only fold fusion does
- **Trap:** MIS from Leydig cells — **Actually:** Sertoli; Leydig make testosterone
- **Trap:** gubernacular swelling androgen-dependent — **Actually:** independent; CSL regression and inguinoscrotal phase dependent
- **Trap:** ascending testis congenital — **Actually:** acquired; processus remnant fails to involute
- **Trap:** remove every streak gonad — **Actually:** remove in 46,XY (gonadoblastoma)
- **Trap:** upper vaginal epithelium Müllerian — **Actually:** replaced by UGE to cervix
- **Trap:** OHVIRA = renal agenesis — **Actually:** usually dysplasia; "RA" = renal anomalies
- **Trap:** lower-pole orifice ectopic — **Actually:** lateral, refluxes; upper pole ectopic
</div>

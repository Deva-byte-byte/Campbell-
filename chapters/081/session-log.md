## Source flags

- **Figure cross-references swapped.** The text cites Fig. 81.1 for the modified templates and Fig. 81.4 for supine steep Trendelenburg. The captions are the other way round: 81.1 is the Trendelenburg position and 81.4 the templates. The documents carry the corrected references.
- **GETUG T93BP attribution.** The trial is cited to Culine et al. (2007), but the 70% relapse and 50% death figures in the same passage are cited to Fox et al. (1993), which predates the trial. This is an internal inconsistency in the source. The documents give the figures as the source states them, attached to the trial.
- **Key Points versus body text on node yield.** The final Key Points box says node yields are "comparable" between open and robotic RPLND. The Controversy section says MI yields are lower than open, and the Efficacy section says they are "approaching" the open benchmark of ≈30. Document 2 carries the reconciled wording: recent series approach the benchmark, historical series were lower.
- **Efficacy paragraph mislabels Table 81.1.** It says the table covers "laparoscopic and open series"; it actually covers laparoscopic and robotic series. Corrected.
- **Table 81.1, LeBlanc 2001 EBL printed "<00".** A digit has been dropped, most likely "<100". The table was rebuilt as "<100–390".
- **Table 81.1 units unstated.** Operative time is read as minutes, EBL as mL, length of stay as days and follow-up as months, consistent with the magnitudes.
- **Table 81.1, robotic rows.** Operative time and conversion are blank for five series (Nason, Taylor, Supron, Rocco, Hiester), and the conversion column reads as bare counts. Ranges were computed from the series that report them.
- **"NSGC"** in Patient and Tumor Factors should read NSGCT.
- **Resequencing.** The two-column layout interrupts two passages. The end of the nerve-sparing paragraph ("…right- and left-sided sympathetic chain…") continues on p. 1710 after Instrumentation, and Future Directions starts beneath the Efficacy Key Points on p. 1714. Both were restored to reading order.
- **Text extraction artefacts, not source errors.** In the extracted text, ">", "<", "≥" and "×" appeared as "!", "#" and a stray quotation mark. The page images show the correct symbols: >1 cm, <5 years, ≥90%, ≥10 mm, BEP ×3, EP ×4.
- **Non-landmark citations removed.** These include the 1992 first laparoscopic and 2006 first robotic case reports, which are kept as dates only. GETUG T93BP is the only landmark trial retained by name.

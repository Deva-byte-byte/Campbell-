# Chapter 159 — Active Surveillance of Prostate Cancer

<div class="top" markdown="1">
<p class="boxt">If you read nothing else</p>

- **Grade group (GG) 1 (Gleason 3+3=6) → active surveillance (AS)**: guideline standard for most
- **GG1 metastatic potential ≈ zero**; metastases = **missed higher grade**
- **Misattribution ~30%** (systematic biopsy) **≫ true progression 1–2%/yr**
- **Intervention ~25% at 5 yr**, whatever the inclusion criteria
- **15-yr prostate cancer mortality (PCM) 0.5% (Hopkins) vs 5% (Toronto)**
- **Epstein: ≤2 cores, no core >50%, PSA density (PSAD) <0.15**
- **Confirm biopsy 1–2 yr; PSA 6-monthly; biopsy every 2–5 yr**
- **GG ≥3 → treat**; **volume or PSA rise → investigate**, don't treat
- **AS = deferred cure (life expectancy ≥10 yr); watchful waiting (WW) = palliation**
- **BRCA2 → consider definitive therapy**, even if low grade
</div>

## OVERDIAGNOSIS AND OVERTREATMENT

- **Prostate overdiagnosis 87–94%** if the whole reservoir were detected
- **US Preventive Services Task Force (USPSTF): 2012 Grade D → 2018 Grade C**, thanks to AS uptake
- **AS → WW when life expectancy <5–10 yr** (≈80 yr, earlier if comorbid)
- **WW: no biopsy, no imaging**; treat symptoms only
- **Within 5 yr, only very aggressive high-grade disease kills**
- **Older WW trial outcomes ≠ modern AS**

## CLINICALLY INSIGNIFICANT CANCER

- **Autopsy prevalence ≈ age as %** (≥80% over 80)
- **Lifetime prostate cancer death risk 3%** (North America)
- **PSA + systematic biopsy → cancer in 15–25%**
- **Harbour : death ≈ 20:1; diagnosis : death ≈ 6:1**
- **Gleason grade group = most powerful predictor**
- **GG2 = 3+4; GG3 = 4+3; GG4 = 8; GG5 = 9–10**
- **Pattern 3 molecularly near-normal**; patterns 4/5 aberrant
- **PTEN: present 90% (G3) vs deleted 70–90% (G4)**
- **PTEN loss alone ≠ metastasis**; **needs MYC amplification**
- **GG1 invades locally** (extraprostatic extension, rarely seminal vesicles) → still "cancer"
- **True progression 10–20% after 10 yr** → lifelong follow-up
- **Undergraded ~30%: 85% 3+4, 10% 4+3, 5% ≥8**
- **Grade progression ≠ automatic treatment** (3+4 at 80 after AS from 65)
- **2005 Gleason revision** → stage migration

**Trials:**

- **SPCG-4** (Scandinavian, pre-PSA): **PCM 19.6% vs 31.3%**, relative risk 0.55, **+2.9 life-years**; benefit **<65 yr and GG ≥3 only**; **4+3 >5× risk of 3+4**
- **PIVOT** (Veterans Affairs): hazard ratio 0.84; **~1 yr gained**; low-risk observation **PCM 2.7%**
- **ProtecT** (UK, PSA-detected): **no PCM or survival difference at 15 yr (all <3%)**; **metastases 9.4% vs 4.5% vs 5.0%**; **61% of monitoring arm treated**
- **ProtecT active monitoring ≠ AS**: no scheduled biopsy
- **ProtecT QoL: monitoring same or better in every domain**; 24.4% alive untreated at 15 yr
- **ASIST: MRI + targeted biopsy — no confirmation-biopsy difference; ~50% less AS failure at 2 yr**

## OUTCOMES OF ACTIVE SURVEILLANCE

- **10 prospective series (~10,000 men); GAP3 (Global Action Plan Prostate Cancer Active Surveillance): >15,000 men, 25 centres**
- **Commonest death on AS: cardiovascular**; non-prostate death **10×** prostate death
- **Toronto: all Gleason 6 + selected intermediate risk; 50% eligible; 5% PCM** (1.5% actual)
- **Hopkins: Epstein only; 20% eligible; 0.5% PCM**
- **Baseline Gleason 7 on AS → ≥20% metastasis at 15 yr**
- **Very low risk = Epstein; low risk = GG1 + PSA <10**
- **High-volume Gleason 6 → still AS**; add MRI, repeat biopsy or genomics
- **Guidelines:** **NICE: MRI at enrolment**; **AUA (American Urological Association): MRI before confirmation biopsy**
- **NICE alone: intermediate risk → radical treatment *or* AS**
- **Neuroendocrine progression without PSA rise** → check testosterone, image
- **Occult high-grade predictors:** PSAD, free/total PSA, cores, core %, age, race, **restricted diffusion**
- **Misattributed upgrades mostly → GG2**; beyond GG3 rare
- **Intermediate risk on AS: ≥20% metastasis, 80% free at 15 yr**

## OTHER RISK FACTORS

- **Black men: unequivocally AS candidates**; consider more intense surveillance
- **Family history inflation** → relatives' low-grade disease still AS
- **First-degree relative with metastases <60 yr → germline testing**
- **BRCA2 > BRCA1, ATM** for upgrading; most BRCA2 upgraded by 10 yr
- **Germline testing (National Comprehensive Cancer Network):** metastatic/high risk; strong family history; **consider if intraductal or cribriform**
- **Confirmation biopsy 1–2 yr** (key points: 1–3 yr; can delay to yr 3)
- **Digital rectal examination every 1–2 yr; MRI ≤ every 18–24 mo**
- **Operate if: GG ≥3** (unless life expectancy short)
- **Don't operate for: Gleason 6 volume rise, PSA rise alone, small-volume GG2**
- **Compliance → better survival**; outcomes assume compliance
- **Diffusion-weighted imaging (DWI): diffusion inversely ∝ Gleason**
- **MRI before confirmation biopsy + systematic cores** (NICE recommends)
- **Serial MRI alone cannot exclude progression** (negative predictive value 0.8–0.9)
- **MRI-invisible cancer → more indolent**
- **ASIST site variation → MRI quality and reader experience matter**
- **Progression risk on AS 0.2–5% at 15 yr** → modest biomarker gain
- **Decipher: 22 genes** — upgrading GG1, not GG2
- **Oncotype DX Genomic Prostate Score: 12 genes, 4 pathways**; mixed on AS
- **Prolaris Cell Cycle Progression: 31 + 15 housekeeping genes**; CCP >2 → higher death

## GENOMICS CAVEATS

- **Heterogeneity**: scores differ between foci in one gland
- **Bayes: 3% prior, 90% specificity → 10% false positive (~3×)**
- **Tissue genomics only if** high volume, high PSAD or GG2
- **Repeat genomic test adds nothing**
- **Genomics never sole basis for AS**

## PSYCHOLOGY AND LIFESTYLE

- **10–15% elect treatment without indication**
- **Anxiety: diagnosis, not AS**; fare poorly: **anxiety disorder, social isolation**
- **Smoking cessation: most cost-effective intervention in medicine**
- **Two minutes of specialist counselling** can change smoking
- **Vigorous exercise >3 h/wk** (≥6× resting metabolic rate); **brisk walking ≥7 h/wk**
- **BMI ≥27.5 (Asian) → adverse pathology**
- **Each +5 kg/m² BMI → higher prostate cancer mortality**
- **Obesity affects later biopsies, not confirmation** → true progression
- **Men's Eating and Living (MEAL) randomised trial (vegetables): no effect at 24 mo**
- **Vitamin D 1000–2000 IU/day** (trial dose 4000 IU/day × 1 yr)
- **Selenium ≥140 µg/day → higher mortality**; **vitamin E (SELECT) → more cancer**

## PHARMACOLOGIC INTERVENTION

- **REDEEM (dutasteride): 38% less "progression"**, volume only; **no less upgrading**
- **5-alpha-reductase inhibitors: not for AS prevention**; fine if benign prostatic hyperplasia indication
- **REDUCE, PCPT (Prostate Cancer Prevention Trial): slight high-grade concern** with 5-alpha-reductase inhibitors
- **Antiandrogen "progression" benefit = cytoreduction**; only upgrading counts
- **Statins reasonable; metformin not routine** (MAST trial pending)
- **Apalutamide 240 mg × 90 d; enzalutamide 160 mg × 1 yr** — investigational

## BARRIERS, NOMENCLATURE, ECONOMICS

- **Shared decision-making: 4 requisites**; physician = **navigator, not driver**
- **Biopsy of 24–38 transperineal cores → erectile dysfunction risk**
- **Self-discontinuation 7–15%**
- **IDLE = indolent lesion of epithelial origin**; "cancer" retained; **GG 1/5 vs 6/10**
- **Non-metastasising cancers: basal cell carcinoma, gliomas**
- **15-yr AS retention 43% (Toronto) / 55% (Hopkins)**
- **Biannual biopsy ≈ surgery cost by 10 yr**
- **Most cost-effective: annual MRI, biopsy only if PI-RADS ≥4**

<p class="boxt">Classic traps</p>

- **Trap:** watchful waiting = AS — **Actually:** WW palliative, no biopsies
- **Trap:** SPCG-4 observation predicts AS outcome — **Actually:** pre-PSA, intermediate–high risk
- **Trap:** ProtecT excess metastases condemn AS — **Actually:** >⅓ higher risk, no protocol biopsies
- **Trap:** Gleason 6 metastasises — **Actually:** metastases = missed higher grade
- **Trap:** Very low vs low risk = metastatic risk — **Actually:** occult higher-grade likelihood
- **Trap:** Gleason 6 volume increase → treat — **Actually:** investigate (MRI, biomarker)
- **Trap:** REDEEM proves dutasteride prevents progression — **Actually:** volume cytoreduction; no upgrading difference
- **Trap:** negative MRI excludes progression — **Actually:** NPV depends on risk
- **Trap:** genomics for every GG1 — **Actually:** low prior → false positives ≈3× true risk
- **Trap:** stable PSA = stable disease — **Actually:** neuroendocrine change if hypogonadal

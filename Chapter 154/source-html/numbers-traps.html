Chapter 154 — Prostate Cancer Biomarkers — Numbers, Gold Standards and Traps

If you read nothing else
  • Prostate-specific antigen (PSA): organ specific, not cancer specific.
  • 5α-reductase inhibitor (5ARI) → PSA ↓50% at 12 months → double it.
  • Percentage free PSA (%fPSA): low = cancer; unchanged by 5ARI.
  • %fPSA approved range: PSA 4–10, negative digital rectal examination (DRE).
  • PSA velocity → never isolated biopsy trigger.
  • PSA density ≤0.15 → classic active surveillance (AS) criterion.
  • Prostate Health Index (PHI) = (p2PSA ÷ fPSA) × √tPSA.
  • 4Kscore cut-off 7.5%; below → low long-term metastasis risk.
  • Six National Comprehensive Cancer Network (NCCN) 2023 biomarkers: IsoPSA, 4Kscore, PHI, SelectMDx, EPI, MPS.
  • BRCA2 → highest risk, younger, higher grade, worse survival.

Introduction

  • PSA discovered 1979; PSA era → most cancers found at T1c.
  • ERSPC (European Randomized Study of Screening for Prostate Cancer) and Göteborg-1: screening ↓ cancer-specific mortality; ↑ negative biopsies, overdiagnosis.
  • USPSTF 2012 (US Preventive Services Task Force): Grade D against screening.
  • Clinically significant = Gleason score (GS) ≥7 = Grade Group (GG) ≥2.
  • Generations: 1st PSA (epithelium) → 2nd PCA3, fusions (cancer) → 3rd SChLAP1 (high grade).
  • First-line screening test: PSA.
  • Guidelines: secondary test (magnetic resonance imaging, MRI, or biomarker) before biopsy if PSA raised.
  • Three settings: early detection; AS vs treatment; post-treatment.

Blood-based biomarkers

  • PSA = human kallikrein 3 (hK3); family 15 serine proteases, chromosome 19q13.
  • Homology with PSA: hK1 60%, hK2 ~78–80%.
  • Pre-pro-PSA (17-aa leader) → proPSA 244 aa (7-aa pro) → hK2 → active PSA 237 aa.
  • Function: liquefies semen — cleaves semenogelin, fibronectin.
  • Source: prostatic luminal epithelial cells; androgen dependent.
  • Half-life: 2–3 days.
  • Tissue PSA peaks: 0–6 months, >10 years; serum detectable from puberty.
  • PSA–A2M: retains some protease activity.
  • Serum: ~70% bound; free 5–45%.
  • PSA–α1-antichymotrypsin (ACT): 60–90% of bound; inactive; detectable.
  • PSA–α2-macroglobulin (A2M): 10–20%; undetectable (all epitopes masked).
  • PSA–α1-protease inhibitor (API): 1–5%; detectable.
  • Elevation mechanism: disrupted basal layer/basement membrane.
  • Most important cause of rise: prostate disease (cancer, benign prostatic hyperplasia [BPH], prostatitis).
  • Primary screening confounders: BPH, prostatitis.
  • After biopsy: raised ≥4 weeks.
  • Cycling >55 km: ~10% rise.
  • Ejaculation, age ≥50: transient rise; baseline within 24 h → abstain, repeat.
  • Per cell: cancer makes less PSA than benign.
  • Per cc serum PSA: Gleason 3 ≈ benign; Gleason 4 several-fold higher.
  • Doubling rule: overestimates first 6 months; underestimates after years.
  • PCPT (Prostate Cancer Prevention Trial): 5ARI improves PSA screening performance.
  • PSA velocity: normal 0.04 ng/mL/yr; BPH 0.07–0.27.
  • PSA density = PSA ÷ volume; biopsy cut-offs 0.08–0.15.
  • Density after negative MRI: 0.15 lacks specific supporting data.
  • Density in AS: key predictor of reclassification.
  • %fPSA FDA indication: total PSA 4–10, negative DRE.
  • NCCN: %fPSA to improve specificity before repeat biopsy.
  • Risk calculators with fPSA: PCPT, Prostate Biopsy Collaborative Group (PBCG).
  • %fPSA thresholds: 15–25%; 25% → 95% sensitivity.
  • %fPSA commonest use: raised PSA after negative biopsy (21–28%).
  • %fPSA reports: any-grade risk → overestimates GG ≥2.
  • Free isoforms (~equal): proPSA, benign PSA (BPSA), intact PSA (iPSA).
  • proPSA: peripheral zone, cancer; [–2]proPSA most useful → PHI.
  • Truncated proPSAs: [–2], [–4], [–5]; p2PSA cleaved Leu5–Ser6.
  • BPSA: transition zone, BPH, volume; no prognostic value.
  • iPSA: used within 4Kscore.
  • IsoPSA: structure-based; K = bottom:top complexed PSA; unaffected by 5ARI, α-blockers.
  • 4Kscore: tPSA + fPSA + iPSA + hK2 + age, prior biopsy, DRE.
  • hK2 vs PSA: hK2 minimal in benign, intense in cancer; independent of PSA.
  • IsoPSA output: KR = % risk GG ≥2 (0–100%).
  • 4Kscore output: % risk GG ≥2; adds ~0.11 area under curve (AUC) to clinical model.
  • 4Kscore 6–15% thresholds: 30–58% fewer biopsies.
  • 4Kscore 7.5%: sensitivity 94%, negative predictive value (NPV) 95%.
  • 4Kscore origin: ERSPC Göteborg (develop) → Rotterdam (validate).
  • PHI thresholds: 24 or 28.6 proposed; none validated.
  • PHI 28.6: 90% sensitivity GG ≥2, ~30% biopsies avoided.
  • PHI reports: any-grade, not GG ≥2, risk.

Urine-based biomarkers

  • PCA3 (prostate cancer antigen 3): long non-coding RNA, chromosome 9q21–22.
  • PCA3 score = (PCA3 mRNA ÷ KLK3 mRNA) × 1000; post-DRE urine.
  • PCA3 vs PSA: independent of prostate size.
  • PCA3 thresholds: 10, 25, 35; nomogram cut-off 17.
  • PCA3 threshold choice: depends on prior biopsy status.
  • PCA3 evidence: beats total PSA; no health-outcome benefit shown.
  • PCA3 FDA/CE indication: prior negative biopsy.
  • PCA3 now: not standalone → inside EPI and MPS.
  • Assay evolution: reverse transcription polymerase chain reaction (RT-PCR) → transcription-mediated amplification (TMA).
  • SelectMDx: DLX1 + HOXC6 mRNA, post-DRE, + clinical; needs prostate volume.
  • SelectMDx performance: sensitivity ~93%, NPV ~95% GG ≥2.
  • ExoDx Prostate IntelliScore (EPI): exosomal PCA3 + ERG + SPDEF; no DRE, no clinical data.
  • EPI thresholds: 15.6 initial; 20 repeat biopsy.
  • EPI 15.6: ~92% sensitivity; ~⅓ biopsies avoided; ~8% GG ≥2 missed.
  • EPI predecessor EXO106: PCA3 + ERG; threshold 10.
  • MyProstateScore (MPS): PCA3 + TMPRSS2:ERG + serum PSA; 0–100.
  • MPS thresholds: 10 initial (97% sensitivity, 98% NPV); 15 repeat.
  • MPS 10: ~⅓ biopsies avoided; 3% GG ≥2 missed.
  • MPS validated: Black men, suspicious DRE.
  • PCA3 + T2:ERG: specificity 17% → 33% at 95% sensitivity.
  • TMPRSS2:ERG (2005): ~100% specific; ~50% of tumours, ~75% of men.
  • TMPRSS2: androgen regulated; partner ERG or ETV1 (ETS family).
  • miRNA: 19–22 nucleotides; miR-141 (aggressive), urine miR-187.

Tissue-based biomarkers

  • Epigenetics: DNA methylation, histone acetylation; CpG islands = GC-rich promoters.
  • Methylated genes: GSTP1, APC, RARβ2, RASSF1A.
  • Field (halo) effect: methylation beside tumour, not distant.
  • ConfirmMDx: GSTP1, APC, RASSF1 → repeat-biopsy NPV ~88–90%.
  • Prolaris: 31 cell cycle progression genes; predicts cancer-specific death.
  • Oncotype Dx Prostate: 12 genes/4 pathways; adverse pathology = primary pattern 4 or pT3.
  • Decipher: 22 markers; post-prostatectomy; ≥0.4 → consider radiotherapy.
  • Decipher risk groups: low → intermediate → high 10-year metastasis.
  • Genomic classifier limit: multifocality; data mostly retrospective.
  • PORTOS: 24 genes; adjuvant radiotherapy response.
  • G-MAJOR: prospective randomised trial, Decipher in favourable-risk.
  • Hereditary share: up to 40–50%; familial 15–20% of cases.
  • One affected relative: 2–4-fold risk.
  • >4 risk SNPs: 4.5-fold; with family history 10-fold.
  • STHLM3: 4 kallikreins + 232 SNPs + clinical.
  • BRCA2: 4.5-fold <65; ~8.6-fold overall. BRCA1: 1.8-fold <65.
  • Lynch MSH2: ~10-fold. CHEK2: OR ~2 (3.4 familial).
  • HOXB13 G84E: 4.5-fold; not more aggressive.
  • Aggressive-disease genes: BRCA1, BRCA2, ATM — DNA repair.
  • NCCN germline focus: BRCA testing.
  • Multigene panel: BRCA1/2, ATM, CHEK2, MLH1, MSH2, TP53, EPCAM.
  • ConfirmMDx, Black men: NPV ~94% for GS ≥7.
Classic traps
  • Trap: cancer cells make more PSA — Actually: less per cell; leak raises serum.
  • Trap: low free PSA reassuring — Actually: low %fPSA signals cancer.
  • Trap: 5ARI invalidates %fPSA — Actually: ratio unchanged.
  • Trap: A2M-bound PSA measured — Actually: undetectable by most assays.
  • Trap: rising velocity → biopsy — Actually: never an isolated trigger.
  • Trap: doubling rule exact — Actually: overestimates early, underestimates late.
  • Trap: one threshold fits all — Actually: repeat biopsy higher (EPI 20, MPS 15).
  • Trap: BPSA marks cancer — Actually: BPH, transition zone.
  • Trap: HOXB13 → aggressive cancer — Actually: risk only, not aggression.
  • Trap: tissue classifier sees whole gland — Actually: sampled focus only.

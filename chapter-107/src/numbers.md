### The numbers to lock in — Instalment 1

- **Key facts and cut-offs**
    - Box 107.1 risk factors: impaired compliance; DESD; DISD; high-pressure DO throughout filling; **DLPP > 40 cm H₂O**; poor emptying with high storage pressure.
    - Antibiotic prophylaxis: **single dose within 1 hour**; **age > 70** → yes.
    - Pre-invasive work-up: validated symptom score, **3-day frequency–volume chart**, free flow, PVR.
    - Voiding efficiency = **VV / (VV + PVR) × 100**; **Pdet = Pves − Pabd**.
    - Adult fill rate **30–100 mL/min**.
- **Gold standards and first-line**
    - Prophylaxis first choice: **oral trimethoprim–sulfamethoxazole**.
    - **Urinalysis before every invasive study.**
    - EMG ICS standard: **two perineal surface electrodes**.
- **Eponyms**: **Wein functional classification (1981)** — storage vs emptying, bladder vs outlet.
- **Prophylaxis — yes vs no (two separate lists)**
    - **Yes**: NLUTD; BOO/elevated PVR; age > 70; asymptomatic bacteriuria; immunosuppression (renal transplant); chronic catheters/CIC; at-risk joint implants; positive urinalysis without UTI.
    - **No**: normal anatomy (level 1); diabetes; recent hospitalisation; obesity/nutrition; menopause; recurrent UTI; gender; prior urological surgery; valvular heart disease (not routinely).
- **When not to test**: **symptomatic UTI → postpone** until treated.
- **Classic traps**
    - Recurrent UTI and valvular disease **do not** warrant prophylaxis; asymptomatic bacteriuria **does**.
    - Risk factor discovered during UDS → prophylaxis **immediately after**, not skipped.
    - Elevated PVR says emptying is poor, **not why**; confirm with a second measurement.
    - Flattened flow = fixed obstruction; interrupted flow ≠ proof of underactivity without pressure.
    - Drug effect question → test **on** medication; aetiology question → consider **stopping** it.

### The numbers to lock in — Instalment 2

- **Key facts and cut-offs**
    - Water-filled vs air-charged Pdet differ by **up to 10 cm H₂O**; urodynamic signals mostly **< 3 Hz**.
    - Water-filled resonates **~10 Hz** (underdamped); air-charged attenuates **> 3 Hz** (overdamped).
    - **Up to 50% of women with UUI show no DO.**
    - Compliance **< 20 mL/cm H₂O** proposed as impaired (10–20 used); ICS points: start of filling and capacity.
    - **Storage pressure > 40 cm H₂O** → upper-tract damage (McGuire).
    - ALPP baseline standing **20–40 cm H₂O**; test from **150 mL**, then every **50 mL**.
- **Gold standards and first-line**
    - Water-filled external transducer = reference for most nomograms; **zero at symphysis pubis**.
    - **Concentric needle EMG** = superior signal; surface = comfort.
    - **Rectal catheter** preferred for Pabd.
    - Infusate: **saline or contrast; never CO₂**.
    - Commonest flowmeters: **load cell and rotating disc**.
- **Classic traps**
    - Air-charged and water-filled **not interchangeable** for absolute values.
    - Load cell needs **urine density set**; rotating disc needs no differentiation.
    - DO subtypes look **identical** on tracing — label comes from history.
    - Sustained low-amplitude IDC mimics poor compliance → **stop filling**.
    - **VUR pop-off makes compliance look better** than it is.
    - ALPP is **total** pressure, not the change from baseline.
    - DO is more frequent **upright** → test in the symptomatic position.

### The numbers to lock in — Instalment 3

- **Key facts and cut-offs**
    - **ALPP < 60 = ISD; 60–90 equivocal; ≥ 90 little/no ISD** (McGuire 1993).
    - Hypermobility: **Q-tip > 30°**.
    - **DLPP > 40 → hydronephrosis/VUR in 85%** of myelodysplastic children (McGuire 1981); **20 cm H₂O more sensitive**.
    - UPP normal women: functional length **~3 cm**, **MUCP 40–60**; **MUCP ≤ 20 = ISD** (type III).
    - Minimum VV **150 mL** (linear above, hyperbolic below); corrected Qmax = **Qmax/√VV**.
    - Bethanechol: fill **1 mL/s to 100 mL**; **2.5 mg SC (0.035 mg/kg)**; **positive ≥ 15 cm H₂O at 30 min**.
    - **BOOI = PdetQmax − 2Qmax: > 40 obstructed, 20–40 equivocal, < 20 unobstructed.**
    - **BCI = PdetQmax + 5Qmax: > 150 strong, 100–150 normal, < 100 weak.**
    - Reproducibility: pressure ±9–14 cm H₂O, Qmax 0.4–2 mL/s.
- **Gold standards**: **PFS is the most accurate assessment of contractility and outlet**; **ICS provisional nomogram/BOOI** for men with BPH-type LUTS; BCI = commonest contractility index.
- **Eponyms**
    - **Brown and Wickham (1969)** — perfused side-hole UPP.
    - **Cannon's law / Lapides (1962)** — denervation supersensitivity.
    - **Castro–Griffiths BOR (1972)** — bladder independent of urethra; **Griffiths URR (1975)** — urethra independent of bladder.
    - **Abrams–Griffiths (1979), URA (1989), Schäfer LinPURR (1990)** — male obstruction nomograms.
    - **Siroky** (men), **Liverpool** (men and women) flow nomograms.
- **Classic traps**
    - ALPP (low = bad) vs DLPP (high = bad).
    - VLPP < CLPP in the same patient; larger catheter → lower ALPP.
    - SIDO mimics SUI — detrusor cause.
    - UPP does not diagnose SUI; ALPP requires SUI.
    - "Areflexia" → **neurogenic acontractile detrusor**; failed void in the laboratory → **situational inability to void as usual**, not acontractile.
    - Voluntary stop test gives the **lowest Piso**.
    - Bethanechol test positive does not predict treatment response.

### The numbers to lock in — Instalment 4

- **Key facts and cut-offs**
    - Unequivocal obstruction needs **Pdet ≥ 40 even at zero flow**; at Qmax 5 → PdetQmax ≥ 50.
    - WF constants **a ≈ 25 cm H₂O, b ≈ 6 mm/s**; Wmax **7.0 W/m²** suggested threshold.
    - ~**two-thirds** of men with BPO have DO; resolves in **50–67%** after relief.
    - MUPP: normal **20–30 cm H₂O drop** across membranous urethra; **> 5 cm H₂O** prostatic drop = obstruction.
    - **Piso < 50 cm H₂O (men) = detrusor underactivity.**
    - Female cut-points: **Qmax ≤ 15/PdetQmax ≥ 20** (Chassagne); **≤ 11/≥ 21** (Lemack–Zimmern); **≤ 12/≥ 25** (Defreitas).
    - **Solomon–Greenwell: PdetQmax − 2.2Qmax < 0 → < 10%; > 5 → > 50%; > 18 → > 90%.**
    - DISD: lesion **above T10–L1**.
    - PBNO **types I (high P/low Q), II (normal P/low Q), III (delayed opening)**.
- **Gold standards**
    - **VUDS = gold standard invasive UDS in NLUTD**; **only way to diagnose PBNO, DISD and bladder neck function**.
    - VUDS more accurate than surface EMG for dysfunctional voiding.
- **Eponyms**
    - **Yalla** — MUPP (diagnoses and localises).
    - **Blaivas–Groutz** — free Qmax vs Pdetmax, four zones, **overdiagnoses**.
    - **Solomon–Greenwell** — VUDS-referenced probability nomogram.
    - **Nitti (1999)** — VUDS criteria for female BOO.
- **Classic traps**
    - **ICS nomogram/BOOI must not be used in women** — underestimates BOO.
    - EMG rise with IDC = guarding reflex, **not** DESD.
    - DESD needs a suprasacral lesion; otherwise = **dysfunctional voiding**.
    - Confirm dysfunctional voiding on **free uroflow**.
    - Type II PBNO has **normal pressure** — missed without fluoroscopy.
    - Low end-filling pressure with VUR ≠ safe bladder.

### The numbers to lock in — Instalment 5

- **Key facts and cut-offs**
    - AUA/SUFU UDS guideline **2012**: **19 statements, four disease states**.
    - **ValUE**: success **76.9% vs 77.2%** — office evaluation **non-inferior**; entry PVR **< 150 mL**; endpoint UDI ≥ 70% reduction.
    - EAU PFS triggers: cannot void **> 150 mL**; **Qmax > 10 mL/s**; **PVR > 300 mL**; age **> 80** or **< 50**.
    - Storage symptoms persist after TURP in **~one-third**.
    - Oxford grades: **A level 1; B level 2/3; C level 4 or expert opinion**.
- **Gold standards and first-line**
    - **Filling cystometry = gold standard for storage disorders.**
    - **VUDS optimum in neuro-urology** (EAU); unavailable → **CMG continuing into PFS**, physiological fill, body-warm saline.
    - **α-Blocker for male LUTS with normal emptying needs no UDS.**
- **Landmark trials**
    - **SISTEr** — Burch vs sling; UDS did not predict outcome.
    - **ValUE** — 630 women; office evaluation alone non-inferior at 1 year.
- **Eponyms**: **Bors and Blinn (1957)** — ice water test (UMN positive, LMN negative).
- **When to test / treat**
    - **Significantly impaired compliance = only absolute urodynamic indication to treat BOO.**
    - **UDS essential in SCI and myelomeningocele** before treatment and in follow-up.
    - UDS **not needed** initially: post-stroke UUI; MS with low PVR; uncomplicated OAB; index SUI patient.
    - NICE 2006 pre-SUI-surgery UDS: suspected DO, prior SUI/anterior prolapse surgery, voiding dysfunction.
    - Occult SUI testing: high-grade POP without SUI → **stress test with reduction**.
- **Classic traps**
    - EAU Qmax trigger is **> 10**, not < 10.
    - AUA SUI 2023: **may omit** UDS in index patients (conditional, grade B).
    - UDS raised surgeon confidence in ValUE but **not** outcomes.
    - IWT must **not precede** standard UDS.
    - NLUTD aim: **low filling pressure over complete emptying**.

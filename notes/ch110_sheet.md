# Chapter 110 — Overactive Bladder — Numbers, Gold Standards and Traps

<div class="box" markdown="1">
If you read nothing else

- **Overactive bladder (OAB) = urgency** ± frequency/nocturia ± urgency urinary incontinence (UUI), **no urinary tract infection (UTI) or other pathology**
- **OAB = symptom; detrusor overactivity (DO) = urodynamic finding**
- **Minimum work-up (American Urological Association/SUFU [AUA/SUFU]):** history, examination, **urinalysis**
- **No urodynamics/cystoscopy/ultrasound** in uncomplicated initial work-up
- **Urodynamics only if result changes invasive/irreversible decision**
- **β3 agonist preferred** (Society of Urodynamics, Female Pelvic Medicine and Urogenital Reconstruction [SUFU]); antimuscarinic >3 months → **10–65% ↑ dementia**
- **Extended-release** antimuscarinics preferred
- **Posterior tibial nerve stimulation (PTNS) ~60–80%; toxin incontinence ↓ ~59%; sacral nerve stimulation (SNS) 5-year ~70–80%**
- **Toxin retention:** ~20% women, **up to 35% men**; **each +10 mL post-void residual (PVR) → ~27% ↑ catheterisation risk**
- **No treatment is acceptable** after counselling
</div>

## Terminology and Definitions

- **Daytime frequency:** **7 voids = upper limit normal**; bother ≥8
- **Nocturia:** **≥1** waking to void; bother **≥2**, marked ≥3
- **Urgency:** sudden, compelling, **difficult to defer**
- **UUI most bothersome** lower urinary tract symptom (LUTS), both sexes
- **Urge incontinence → renamed urgency incontinence**
- **Mixed UI (MUI) = stress UI (SUI) + UUI**; SUI + "OAB dry" ≠ MUI
- **Urinary + faecal = double/dual incontinence**
- **OAB fear = leakage; bladder pain syndrome (BPS) fear = pain**
- **BPS:** pain rises steadily; **no UUI**

## Epidemiology and Economics

- **OAB 11.8–28.7%; UUI 1.5–17.3%**
- **NOBLE (National Overactive Bladder Evaluation):** OAB 16.5% (similar by sex); **UUI 7.6% women vs 2.6% men**
- **EpiLUTS:** highest (28.7%); **EPIC (International Continence Society definitions):** lowest (11.8%)
- **Women:** OAB 1.5–2×, **UUI 2–3×** men
- **Age:** women rise 40s, men 50s–60s; **>75 years ~40% men, ⅓ women**
- **Highest prevalence: Black men and women**
- **Incidence 3–8%/year**; **dry → wet ~1%/year**; **remission ~6%/year**
- **<6% take medication**
- **~92% stop antimuscarinic within 2 years**; half never refill
- **Stopping reasons:** unmet expectations > side effects > cost
- **Most cost-effective third-line: onabotulinumtoxinA 100 U**; **SNS costliest**
- **US cost ~$66 billion (2007)**

## Pathophysiology and Aetiology

- **DO:** only ~half have urgency; **urgency → DO in 44–69%**
- **No animal model of OAB**
- **Main regulatory level: midbrain/brainstem (periaqueductal grey, pontine micturition centre)**
- **Aδ = small myelinated**, volume sensing; **C = unmyelinated, "silent"**, sensitised in OAB
- **Two bladder theories:** **urothelium-based** vs **myogenic** (patchy denervation, micromotions)
- **Bowel–bladder cross-talk:** constipation worsens OAB
- **Bladder outlet obstruction (BOO) not direct cause** — similar OAB prevalence in women
- **Anxiety → ↑ temporal summation** (central sensitisation)

## Clinical Assessment

- **Antimuscarinic relative contraindications:** uncontrolled closed-angle glaucoma, cognitive impairment, retention, gastric stasis
- **β3 relative contraindications:** uncontrolled hypertension, arrhythmia
- **Occult neurology clue:** new OAB + **erectile dysfunction or tremor**
- **Faecal incontinence ~50%** of women with OAB
- **Pelvic/genital ± digital rectal examination = most important examination**
- **Frailty:** **Timed Up and Go**; cognition: **Mini-Mental State Examination**
- **PVR not needed** before first-line/oral therapy if uncomplicated; **bladder scanner** when needed
- **Diary 3–7 days; minimum 24–48 h**
- **Max voided volume = functional capacity**
- **International Consultation on Incontinence Questionnaire (ICIQ) diary sensation: 0 social, 1 normal, 2 urgency passed, 3 urgency reached toilet dry**
- **AUA/SUFU questionnaires:** Urogenital Distress Inventory (UDI), UDI-6, Incontinence Impact Questionnaire (IIQ), **OAB-q**
- **ICIQ-OAB 4 items; OAB-V8 = screening**
- **Lower Urinary Tract Research Network Symptom Index:** both sexes; 29 and 10 items
- **DO correlates best with UUI, worst with nocturia**
- **OAB wet men → DO nearly all; women with UUI: DO absent 30–40%**
- **Severe low-volume DO hiding SUI → refill supine**
- **DO after transurethral resection of prostate up to ~60%**

## Management

- **Fluid ↓ 25%** meaningfully helps
- **Caffeine ↓** helps urgency/frequency, not consistently UI
- **Bariatric surgery:** ~7–8% ↓ UI risk per 5% weight lost
- **Pelvic floor muscle therapy (PFMT)/bladder training:** UI ↓ 50–80%
- **SUFU preferred:** β3, extended-release trospium, darifenacin, fesoterodine
- **Men with voiding LUTS: α1-blocker first-line**; add antimuscarinic/β3 for storage
- **Combination retention risk up to ~3%** → caution if PVR raised
- **PTNS:** weekly × **12 weeks**; response weeks 8–10
- **Toxin:** UTI ~20%; ~¼ completely dry
- **SNS revision >30% at 5 years**; battery replacement inevitable
- **Trial duration before judging efficacy: 4–12 weeks**
- **Augmentation/diversion:** last resort, little non-neurogenic data
- **International Consultation on Incontinence (ICI) pathway, men:** treat **major component** (BOO) first
- **ICI pathway women:** treat **most bothersome** symptom first
- **Frail elderly:** active case finding; treat comorbidity; **wet checks/frequency–volume chart if nocturia**

<div class="traps" markdown="1">
Classic traps

- **Trap:** OAB needs DO on urodynamics — **Actually:** symptom diagnosis
- **Trap:** SUI plus urgency = MUI — **Actually:** MUI needs UUI
- **Trap:** Urinary + faecal = mixed incontinence — **Actually:** double incontinence
- **Trap:** BOO causes OAB, so TURP cures it — **Actually:** DO persists in many
- **Trap:** Urodynamics for every OAB patient — **Actually:** only if decision-changing
- **Trap:** PVR before starting oral drugs — **Actually:** only if complicated
- **Trap:** Absent DO in woman with UUI excludes OAB — **Actually:** absent in 30–40%
- **Trap:** AUA/SUFU lines must be followed in order — **Actually:** not linear; combine
- **Trap:** C fibres normally signal fullness — **Actually:** Aδ do; C silent
- **Trap:** Any prevalence comparison valid — **Actually:** definitions differ across studies

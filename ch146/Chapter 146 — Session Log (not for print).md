## Source-flag appendix

### Systematic character substitution (the key, stated once)
The PDF's text layer uses substitute characters throughout. The meaning of each was confirmed against the page images:
- `"` = **>** (and **β** in "11"-hydroxylase")
- `#` = **>** in thresholds (">50%", ">20%", ">5.0 µg/dL", ">95%") but **−** in formulas ("Enhanced # delayed")
- `&` = **<**; `)` = **≥** ("≥10 HU", "≥150/≥100")
- `(` and `$` = **=** in sample sizes ("N ( 229", "N $ 2005")
- `!` = **+** (Na!, K!, the !!! intensity marks in Table 146.3, "eBooks!") and **α** (17!-hydroxylase, "!-Blockade"); also **~** in Fig. 146.14 ("!1-cm")
- `$` = **α** (receptors, "$-methyltyrosine", "17$-hydroxyprogesterone")
- `%` = **β** (receptors, 11%-hydroxylase, 17%-estradiol, 6%-iodomethyl)
- `'` = **×**

All values are carried in corrected form.

### Source errors, corrected in the notes
1. **Fig. 146.7 legend**: gives the relative washout as 60%. With 15 / 55 / 27 HU, RPW = (55 − 27)/55 = **≈51%**. The absolute washout of 70% is correct. The conclusion (adenoma) stands.
2. **LD-DST, p. 3128**: says the 5.0 µg/dL threshold has "up to an 18% false-*positive* rate". The next paragraph, and the logic of a high cut-off, make it **false-negative**. Corrected.
3. **NP59, p. 3127**: called a "cortisol analogue". It is a **cholesterol analogue**, as the chapter itself says on p. 3121. Corrected.
4. **NP59 planar scintigraphy, p. 3122**: "characterization of lesions **greater** than 2 cm is limited". This is almost certainly **smaller** than 2 cm: SPECT-CT is cited for detecting *smaller* lesions, and p. 3127 gives poor detection below 1.5 cm. The notes say "limited for small lesions".
5. **"Prolonged QP interval"** → **QT interval**.
6. **"KCNJ"** (FH-III gene) → **KCNJ5** (dropped digit).
7. **Table 146.6**: VHL locus "3p25.26" → **3p25–26**. "Cutaneous lichen" and "Amyloidosis" are printed as two separate features; they are one entity, **cutaneous lichen amyloidosis**.
8. **Box 146.6 (Weiss)**: ">5 mitoses per high-power field" → **>5 per 50 high-power fields**. Also "Furman grade" → **Fuhrman**.
9. **Adjuvant radiotherapy in ACC, p. 3151**: "5-year local recurrence rates of 11% and 86%, *without and with* adjuvant radiation". The labels are reversed, since radiation *decreases* recurrence. Correct reading: **≈86% without, ≈11% with**.
10. **Metyrosine, p. 3143**: "extrapyramidal symptoms … necessitate cessation of **phenoxybenzamine**". The paragraph is about metyrosine; corrected to **metyrosine**.
11. **"Hypogonadal hypogonadism"** (p. 3154 text and Fig. 146.30) → **hypogonadotropic hypogonadism**. The same paragraph describes low gonadotropins.
12. **"MOA enzyme"** (p. 3140) → **MAO**.
13. **"Development and hemostasis of the adrenal gland"** (p. 3112) → **homeostasis**.
14. **"LDD-ST"** (p. 3129) → **LD-DST**. **"High-dexamethasone suppression testing"** (p. 3130) → high-**dose**.
15. **p. 3134**: "0 of 883 patients with adrenal lesions, 10 HU" — the comparator symbol was dropped at the line break. Read as **<10 HU**, consistent with p. 3120.
16. **Cross-reference, p. 3133**: "As discussed earlier (see Box 146.6)". Box 146.6 actually appears *later*, in the ACC section.
17. **Citation mismatches** (citations stripped from the notes anyway): Osler's 1896 treatment is cited to Oliver and Sharpey-Schafer 1895; Liddle's description of ectopic ACTH is cited to Meador 1962; "Seyle" should be **Selye**; "Berutti 2022" should be **Berruti**.

### Internal inconsistencies (both values tabulated or stated, with the reason)
- **Mineralocorticoid-receptor-antagonist washout before ARR**: "at least 4 weeks" (Endocrine Society 2016) versus "approximately 6 weeks" (Young; Figs 146.11 and 146.13). Both are given.
- **Phenoxybenzamine postural hypotension**: the text accepts systolic **>80 mmHg**; Fig. 146.22 accepts **>90 mmHg**. Both are given.
- **Extra-adrenal phaeochromocytoma**: "1–25%" (p. 3137) versus 10–25% (Fig. 146.20) and "up to 25%" (p. 3139). "1–25%" is probably a dropped digit, so the notes use 10–25%.
- **Hereditary phaeochromocytoma**: "nearly one-third", "up to 30%" and "more than one-third" all appear. The notes give "about one-third".
- **ACC feminisation**: 1–3% (text) versus 6–10% (Table 146.9). Other ACC hormone percentages also differ between text and table. The table uses all ACCs as its denominator; the text uses functional tumours or all patients, from different series. Both columns are tabulated.
- **Urinary fractionated metanephrine specificity**: 45% in sporadic cases (Table 146.8, the Lenders 2002 cohort) versus ≈91% (Perry 2007, tandem mass spectrometry). The difference is in assay method and population; both are stated.
- **UFC cut-offs in pregnancy**: "twofold to threefold" (p. 3129) versus "approximately threefold" (p. 3130). The notes give 2–3-fold.

### Ambiguities
- **Table 146.12**: each prevalence band is printed beside only one row. Bands were assigned on the assumption that each value heads its group. This matches the text-layer order but is not certain.
- **Table 146.16**: the half-life cells for cortisone acetate, prednisolone, methylprednisolone and triamcinolone are blank in the source. They are left blank ("—") and not inferred.
- **Named landmark trial**: the randomised CT-versus-AVS trial (Dekkers 2016) is not named in the text. The notes name it **SPARTACUS**.
